# Numbers

Made by `node engine/worker/tools/gen-lists.mjs`. Do not edit by hand.

The 44 numbers defined in engine/worker/src/numbers/catalog.js. The screens read the same list from GET /v1/metrics/definitions.

| Id | Name | Unit | Returns | What it means | Formula | Source | Old keys |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gross_sales | Gross sales | money | before | What the products sold for at full price, before anything is taken off. No discounts, returns, shipping or tax. | price times quantity for every item ordered | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalNewGrossSales | gross |
| discounts | Discounts | money | na | How much came off the full price through codes and sale prices. | gross sales + shipping charged + tax charged - order revenue, never below 0 | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out from four measures | discounts |
| returns | Returns | money | na | Money given back to customers for returned or refunded orders in the period. | order revenue - total sales - (tax charged - tax kept), never below 0 | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out from four measures | returns |
| order_revenue | Order revenue (before returns) | money | before | Everything customers paid for orders placed in the period, with shipping and tax, after discounts. Returns are still in it. | gross sales - discounts + shipping charged + tax charged | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalSales |  |
| total_sales | Total sales (after returns) | money | after | Everything customers paid, with shipping and tax, after discounts and after returns. This is the store's own "total sales" line. | order revenue - returns | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure netSales (the tracker titles it Total Sales) | total_sales |
| tax | Tax | money | after | Sales tax collected for the state, after tax on returned orders is given back. It is not the brand's money, so it never counts as revenue. | tax charged - tax refunded | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalNetTaxes | tax, taxes |
| shipping | Shipping charged | money | na | What customers paid for shipping. It is already inside total sales and revenue, so it is never added again. | the shipping lines of every order | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalShippingPrice | ship_rev, shipping |
| revenue | Revenue (after returns) | money | after | The headline sales number: what customers paid after discounts and returns, with shipping, without tax. Every page that says sales or revenue shows this one. | total sales (after returns) - tax | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measures netSales and totalNetTaxes | sales, revenue |
| revenue_before_returns | Revenue (before returns) | money | before | The same headline number with returns still in it. Kept as its own number so the two are never mixed. No page shows it today. | order revenue - tax charged | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measures totalSales and totalTaxes |  |
| net_sales | Net sales | money | after | Product sales alone: after discounts and returns, without shipping and without tax. | revenue - shipping charged | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | net_sales |
| orders | Orders | count | na | How many orders were placed in the period. | a count of orders | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalOrders | orders |
| new_customers | New customers | count | na | Orders from people buying from the brand for the first time. One first order is one new customer. | a count of first orders | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure newCustomersOrders | new_orders |
| returning_customers | Returning customers | count | na | Orders from people who had bought before. | orders - new customers | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | returning_orders |
| new_share | New customer share | share | na | The part of revenue that came from first time customers. | new customer sales divided by (new customer sales + returning customer sales) | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measures newCustomerSales and rcRevenue | new_share |
| new_revenue | New customer revenue | money | after | Revenue from first time customers. | revenue times new customer share | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | new_rev, new_revenue |
| returning_revenue | Returning customer revenue | money | after | Revenue from people who had bought before. | revenue times (1 - new customer share) | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | ret_rev, returning_revenue |
| aov | Average order value | money | after | What one order is worth on average. | revenue divided by orders | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | aov |
| new_aov | New customer order value | money | after | What a first order is worth on average. | new customer revenue divided by new customers | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | new_aov |
| units | Units | count | na | How many items were ordered. | a count of items across all orders | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalOrdersCombinedItemsQuantity | units |
| units_per_order | Units per order | ratio | na | How many items are in an order on average. | units divided by orders | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | upo |
| email_revenue | Email revenue | money | na | Sales the email tool takes credit for. | the email tool's placed order sales | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure klaviyoPlacedOrderSales | email_rev, email |
| email_share | Email share | share | na | The part of revenue the email tool takes credit for. | email revenue divided by revenue | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | email_share |
| sessions | Sessions | count | na | Visits to the store. An estimate, worked out from ad spend and the tracker's cost per visit. | for each day: ad spend divided by cost per session, then added up | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measures blendedAds and pixelCostPerSession | sessions |
| conversion | Conversion rate | share | na | The part of visits that ended in an order. | orders divided by sessions | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, worked out | conversion |
| spend_all | Ad spend | money | na | Everything spent on ads across every platform, plus extra ad costs the team marked as ad spend. | the tracker's blended ad spend (or Meta + Google when it is missing) + extra ad costs | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure blendedAds. Meta spend from daily_insights and Google from ga_adCost as the fallback. Extra costs from the expenses table | spend |
| platform_spend | Platform ad spend | money | na | What the ad platforms alone charged, without extra ad costs. | ad spend - extra ad costs | the same rows as ad spend | base_spend |
| ad_expense | Extra ad costs | money | na | Costs the team added by hand and marked "counts as ad spend", such as a creator fee. | the expenses marked as ad spend, spread over their days | the expenses table | ad_expense |
| spend | Spend (one platform) | money | na | What one ad platform charged in the period. | the platform's own spend, added up by day | the platform's daily rows in the shared database (Meta: daily_insights) | meta_spend, google_spend |
| mer | MER | ratio | after | Dollars of revenue for each dollar of ad spend, for the whole store. It does not care which ad got the credit. | revenue divided by ad spend | revenue and ad spend, as defined here | mer |
| new_customer_mer | New customer MER | ratio | after | Dollars of first time customer revenue for each dollar of ad spend. It shows whether ads are finding new buyers. | new customer revenue divided by ad spend | new customer revenue and ad spend, as defined here | amer |
| roas | ROAS | ratio | na | Dollars of revenue credited to ads on one platform for each dollar spent there. The credit comes from the tracker, not from the platform. | revenue credited to the ads divided by their spend | the tracker's ad credit rows (tw_ad_attr, last platform click) and the platform's spend | roas |
| cac | CAC | money | na | What it cost in ads to win one new customer. | ad spend divided by new customers | ad spend and new customers, as defined here | cac |
| cpa | Cost per order | money | na | What one ad, ad set or platform spent for each order credited to it. | spend divided by the orders credited to it | the tracker's ad credit rows and the platform's spend | cpa |
| cpm | CPM | money | na | What a thousand ad views cost. | spend divided by impressions, times 1,000 | the ad platform's own delivery rows | cpm |
| ctr | CTR | share | na | The part of ad views that were clicked. | clicks divided by impressions | the ad platform's own delivery rows | ctr |
| cogs | Product cost | money | na | What the goods that were sold cost to make or buy. | the brand's own cost rule when one applies on the day, else the tracker's product cost | the cost rules table, else the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalProductCosts | cogs |
| ship_cost | Shipping cost | money | na | What it cost the brand to ship the orders. | the brand's own shipping rule when one applies on the day, else the tracker's shipping cost | the cost rules table, else the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalShippingCosts | ship_cost |
| handling | Handling | money | na | Pick and pack fees. | the tracker's handling fees | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalHandlingFees | handling |
| fees | Payment fees | money | na | What the payment companies kept. | the tracker's payment gateway costs | the tracker rows in the shared database (tw_daily), one value per brand, day and measure, measure totalPaymentGatewayCosts | fees |
| gross_profit | Profit before ads | money | after | What is left of revenue after the costs that rise with every order: product, shipping, handling and payment fees. Ads are not taken off yet. | revenue times the margin set for the month when one is set, else revenue - product cost - shipping cost - handling - payment fees | revenue and the four costs, as defined here. The month margin comes from the brand's goals | gross_profit |
| margin | Margin | share | after | The part of revenue that is left before ads. | profit before ads divided by revenue | worked out | margin |
| profit_after_ads | Profit after ads | money | after | What is left after the costs of every order and after ad spend. Rent, pay and software are not in it. | profit before ads - ad spend | worked out | cm |
| fixed_costs | Fixed costs | money | na | Costs that do not rise with orders, added by hand: rent, pay, software, retainers. | the expenses not marked as ad spend, spread over their days | the expenses table | fixed |
| net_profit | Net profit | money | after | What is left after everything: order costs, ad spend and fixed costs. | profit after ads - fixed costs | worked out | net |
