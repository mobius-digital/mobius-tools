**Patterns**
- **The print is the hero.** The loud print filling the frame stops the scroll. Jokes on top of a small product lose.
- **Real beats staged.** Handheld show-and-tell and real on-course drinking footage win; posed stock-looking people and meme wrappers lose.
- **Movement plus text in frame one** gives the best hook rates (mascot waved at the lens, shaky close-up).
- **Anti-boring and review cards work** ("Nothing against Dad", "retired my old polos").

**What we keep repeating (the sameness problem)**
- Nearly every video is **one guy holding a product up to the lens at the same shop wall** (the neon "Golf is a Drinking Sport" sign). TikTok, Nick and TRYBE ads all look alike.
- **No faces, no friends, no round.** Almost no videos show a real foursome, a reaction, a gift, a partner, or the 19th hole.
- Hat-only videos running polo copy (Nick batch, TRYBE hat ad).
- One body copy on all 11 TikTok videos ("Main Character Energy") and one on all 6 Nick videos.
- A/B/C templates where only the polo changes.
- Copy errors to fix: 330 B says "Golf Sock's Polos" (another client), 332 B copy describes a different print, 336 says 4.99 and 4.91, "Fariway", "Polois", "Wifes".

### 6. Personas

**Main buyer: the Weekend Grunk.** Man 30 to 55, mid to high handicap, plays Saturdays with the same 3 buddies, drinks on the course, has a dad bod and owns it.
- Fears: looking like a try-hard, a shirt that clings to his gut, being the boring guy in navy.
- Wants: to be the guy the group notices, a shirt that fits and breathes, laughs over the score.
- Where he is: Instagram and Facebook reels, TikTok golf comedy, the group chat, the course bar.
- Buys when: he sees a print he has not seen before, sees it on a real guy his size, and hears "where did you get that".

**Smaller personas**
1. **The Crew Captain.** Organises the scramble team, the member-guest pair, the bachelor or guys' trip. Fears the group looking like a mess or someone showing up in the wrong shirt. Wants matching loud polos for the photo. Buys several at once (the $330 to $400 orders). Trigger: an upcoming event and a multi-buy deal.
2. **The Gift Buyer.** Wife, girlfriend, son or daughter buying for a golfer who "has everything". Fears the wrong size or a gift he will not wear. Wants a reaction. Where: Facebook and Instagram, November to December and before Father's Day. Trigger: "finally found something he didn't have" plus easy returns.
3. **The Collector (Drinking Club member).** Owns 8 to 18 polos, watches the Whatnot shows, buys every drop. Fears missing a limited print. Wants the new one first. Trigger: a new drop, restock, members-only gear.
4. **The Big Guy.** 2XL to 5XL, burned by brands that run small and short. Fears skin-tight fabric and shirts that ride up. Wants a polo that actually fits a beer belly. Trigger: seeing a big guy in it, "small through five", "dad bod approved".
5. **The Party-Golf Fan.** Goes to the WM Phoenix Open, Masters watch parties, Topgolf and simulator nights; golf is a social event more than a sport. Fears showing up underdressed for the party. Trigger: the tournament calendar (Masters, Phoenix, 4th of July).
6. **Dad and Lil Dude.** Father who golfs with his kid. Wants matching polos. Trigger: Father's Day and summer.
