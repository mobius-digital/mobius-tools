# Cross-channel: the brand is one system

The most important file. Read it before explaining ANY revenue change, before moving budget between channels, and before calling a channel a winner or a loser. A channel's number is a view of the system, not a separate business. Companion: `measurement-budget.md` (definitions of MER, aMER, NC-CAC, marginal return, noise band).

## 1. How it works now (2025-2026)

### Two jobs: create demand, harvest demand
| Role | Channels | What it does | Where its effect shows up | Click attribution makes it look |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Create | Meta prospecting (the Sales campaign is mostly this), TikTok, YouTube, creators, PR | puts the brand in front of people who were not looking | its own clicks, then brand search, direct, email sign-ups, Amazon, returning revenue later | worse than it is |
| Harvest | Google brand search, Shopping/PMax on brand and retargeting, Meta retargeting share, email, SMS, direct, organic brand | catches people who already want the brand | the last click before the order | better than it is |
| Both | Google non-brand Shopping/PMax, non-brand search | catches category intent (someone searching "forged wedge") and introduces the brand | its own clicks; some brand search later | about right |
| Retain | email and SMS flows, post-purchase, loyalty | makes the second and third order | returning revenue, 30 to 120 days out | about right on flows, inflated on campaigns |

- Harvest channels cannot grow beyond the demand that exists. When harvest channels "decline", the cause is usually upstream in create.
- Meta now does both jobs inside one Advantage+ Sales campaign: it serves existing customers and warm audiences unless told otherwise. Check Meta's audience segment breakdown (new, engaged, existing) before calling Meta "prospecting". A rising existing-customer share means Meta is drifting toward harvest.

### The measured facts behind the system (2025-2026)
- Meta's effect is near-immediate: in 640 Haus tests, 96% of account-wide studies showed significant lift by the test midpoint, plus about 26% more in the post-test window. Meta lifts the primary KPI about 19% on average.
- TikTok and YouTube are slow: TikTok lift is flat in the first half of a test, then rises 247% in the second half and adds 68% after the test ends. 62% of TikTok's lift lands on new customers. Judge them on 3 to 4 weeks, never 7 days.
- Off-site halo: for brands with 25%+ of sales off-site, about 32% of Meta's impact lands off-site; TikTok drove an extra 34% to Amazon and 57% to retail. A brand that sells on Amazon must count Amazon in every read.
- Brand search mostly captures existing demand: the post-test window added under 10% (Haus). When few competitors bid on the brand, only about 9% of its reported value is incremental; with 3+ competitors, about 37%. Pausing brand search shifted about 5% of sales from Amazon to DTC, with Amazon down about 1.9%.
- Paid brand vs organic brand: a brand-search click that disappears when you pause the ad mostly comes back as an organic click on the #1 organic result. Sum paid + organic brand clicks before judging.
- Upper-funnel Meta: mid-funnel campaigns had 70% omnichannel halo vs 46% for conversion campaigns, and higher new-customer share (78% vs 67%). Reach and awareness objectives carried a 6.0x incrementality multiplier vs their reported numbers (Haus).
- Email and SMS do not create new customers from nothing: they convert sign-ups that paid and organic traffic brought in. List growth is a lagging output of traffic.

### What changed recently
- Meta's March 2026 attribution change (click = link click only) reduced Meta-reported conversions; harvest channels did not change. A cross-channel comparison on Meta-reported numbers across March 2026 is invalid. Use TW.
- Advantage+ Sales consolidation (2025) means Meta's spend split between new and existing customers is the algorithm's choice. Audit it monthly.
- Google PMax brand exclusions exist; PMax including brand inflates PMax and cannibalises the brand campaign.
- Klaviyo's 5-day open attribution plus Apple Mail Privacy Protection means Klaviyo claims orders that paid channels started. Klaviyo totals overlap paid totals.

## 2. Decision rules

### The governing rules
1. Judge every channel change on the total first: store revenue, new-customer orders, contribution margin. A channel's own number is the second question.
2. Never fund a harvest channel by cutting a create channel because the harvest channel's ROAS is higher. That moves credit, then a few weeks later moves revenue down.
3. Change one channel at a time, and record the baseline of the other channels before the change (list in section 4).
4. Wait for the slowest channel's lag before a verdict: Meta 7 to 14 days, Google brand and direct 1 to 3 weeks, email list effects 2 to 4 weeks, TikTok and YouTube 3 to 5 weeks, returning revenue 4 to 12 weeks.
5. A change inside the noise band (2/sqrt(weekly orders)) gets no story. "Within noise" is a valid answer.
6. If channel X's attributed revenue moved but store revenue and new-customer orders did not, call it a credit shift and take no budget action.
7. When two channels claim the same orders (journey overlap above 30% in TW order journeys), fund the one that appears first in the journey.

### Lag table: a change in one channel, where it shows up and when
| Change | Same day to 3 days | 4 to 14 days | 2 to 6 weeks | 1 to 3 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta spend +20 to 30% | Meta TW orders up; CPMs and frequency shift | GSC brand impressions and clicks up; Google brand conversions up; direct sessions up; email and SMS sign-ups up | welcome-flow revenue up; abandoned-cart flow up | returning-customer revenue up |
| Meta spend cut 30%+ | Meta TW orders down roughly with spend | brand search and direct start drifting down | brand search, direct, flows clearly lower; total new customers down | returning revenue sags; MER looked better at first, CM dollars down |
| Meta creative fatigue (frequency up, CTR down) | CPA up | brand search flat-to-down, new sign-ups down | aMER falling while MER holds on returning revenue | returning base shrinks |
| TikTok launched or scaled | little in TikTok last click | brand search and direct creep up | lift visible in blended new customers (if big enough); Amazon if listed | some repeat |
| Google brand bid cut or paused | paid brand clicks down, organic brand clicks up | total brand clicks within about 5% if uncontested | competitors may take position if they bid | none, if uncontested |
| PMax brand exclusion added | PMax reported ROAS drops sharply | brand campaign conversions up, PMax new-customer share up | blended new customers flat or up | none |
| Big email campaign (discount) | spike in orders; Klaviyo and Meta both claim them | dip in full-price orders for 3 to 7 days (pull forward) | discount-trained list if repeated weekly | AOV and margin erosion |
| Email list or flow broken | flow revenue down | returning orders down | returning revenue down 10 to 25% | repeat rate down |
| Site, stock or price change | CVR moves on ALL channels at once | CPA moves on every platform together | | |
| Sitewide sale / BFCM | every channel up, CPMs up | post-sale dip 1 to 2 weeks | | returning revenue up from sale cohort (often lower quality) |
- Lag ranges are practitioner estimates built on the Haus timing findings above; refine per brand from its own history.

### Real growth vs credit moving between channels
Apply all five checks; three or more pointing the same way decides it.
1. Did store revenue or new-customer orders move by more than the noise band? No = credit shift.
2. Over-attribution ratio: sum of platform-claimed revenue (Meta-reported + Google-reported + Klaviyo + TikTok) / store revenue. Track weekly. Ratio up with store flat = credit shift. Typical range 1.3 to 1.8.
3. Model spread: channel X's share under first click vs last platform click. If X gained under last click but not first click, it is closing journeys others started.
4. Journey overlap: share of X's orders where TW order journeys show another paid channel earlier. Rising overlap = X is harvesting.
5. New-customer share of X's orders (TW new vs returning by channel; Meta audience segments): real growth in a create channel shows up as new customers, not returning.

### Halo and cannibalisation: rules of thumb
- Meta to brand search: expect brand search clicks to move about 0.3 to 0.6 of Meta's percentage spend change, within 1 to 2 weeks (practitioner range; fit per brand). If Meta rose 30% and brand clicks did not move, Meta's new spend is reaching people who do not remember the brand: a creative or targeting problem, not a search problem.
- Paid brand vs organic brand: cannibalisation is near total when the brand is #1 organically and no competitor bids. Defend only when competitors appear in auction insights.
- PMax vs brand campaign: PMax with brand terms reports high ROAS by eating brand queries. Exclude brand unless AOV is above about $238.
- Email vs Meta retargeting: both claim the warm buyer. When email sends rise and Meta's existing-customer share is high, one of them is paying twice for the same order.
- Discount email vs full-price days: a 20%-off send pulls 3 to 7 days of full-price demand forward. Judge a promo on the 2 weeks around it, not the day.
- Creators: whitelisted creator ads count in Meta; unpaid creator posts show up only in brand search, direct and first click. A creator push with no Meta budget change and rising brand search is the creator.

## 3. Diagnostics

### The method: diagnose any drop or rise in revenue (run in this order, always)
1. **Is the data real?** TW spend vs each platform's own API (Data Health tab: a platform can silently drop out of `blendedAds`); TW summary window shift; Shopify orders vs TW orders; pixel or CAPI breakage (Meta event match quality, Events Manager); Mobius Pulse for platform outages. Stop here if the data is wrong.
2. **Is it bigger than noise?** Compare to the noise band, the 4-week same-weekday baseline and the same weeks last year. Check the calendar (holiday, payday, weather for golf, festival dates for patches, last year's promo date).
3. **Split the number.** Revenue = orders x AOV. Orders = new + returning. If AOV moved: product mix, discount depth, bundles, price change. If returning moved: go to email, SMS, launches, the retention calendar. If new moved: continue.
4. **Demand or conversion?** New orders = sessions x conversion rate. GA4: sessions by channel and landing page; CVR overall and by device. CVR down on every channel at once = site, stock, price, shipping, checkout, a broken landing page. Sessions down in one channel = that channel.
5. **Paid inputs.** Per platform: spend, CPM, CTR, hook and hold, frequency, CPC, click to session match (GA4 sessions vs platform clicks; a gap over 30% = tracking or landing problem). Price of attention (CPM) vs quality of creative (CTR, hook) vs post-click (CVR).
6. **Demand signals.** GSC brand clicks and impressions, Google brand campaign impressions and impression share, direct sessions, new email and SMS sign-ups, Amazon brand traffic if relevant. These are the system's memory of the create channels.
7. **Credit check.** Run the five real-vs-shift checks above. Compare TW last platform click with first click and linear all for the channels that moved.
8. **What did we change?** Meta change log, Locus "What we did" note, Klaviyo send calendar, Shopify price, stock and theme changes, Google change history, last 14 days. Most drops have a cause on this list.
9. **Outside world.** Competitor promos (Meta Ad Library, auction insights), seasonality, news, weather, CPM trend vs last year.
10. **One cause, one fix, one disproof.** State the most likely cause, the fix, the number that would prove you wrong, and when you will look again.

### Decision tree: "revenue is down"
```
Revenue down vs baseline
|
+-- Data broken? (TW spend vs platform API, Shopify vs TW orders, pixel, outage)
|     yes -> fix data, rerun. Do not change spend.
|
+-- Inside noise band and in line with last year's same weeks?
|     yes -> no action. Report "within noise". Look again in 7 days.
|
+-- AOV down, orders flat?
|     -> discount depth, mix shift to cheaper SKU, bundle broken, hero out of stock -> fix offer or stock
|
+-- Returning orders down, new flat?
|     -> Klaviyo: flows live? send volume vs last month? deliverability (open, bounce, spam)?
|        SMS volume? last year had a launch or promo here?
|        -> restore flows / send calendar; plan a retention send
|
+-- New orders down
      |
      +-- CVR down on every channel at once?
      |     -> site speed, checkout, stock-out, price change, shipping threshold, landing page broken
      |        -> fix site; do NOT cut ad spend (ads are not the cause)
      |
      +-- Sessions down, CVR flat
            |
            +-- Paid sessions down
            |     +-- spend down? -> was it planned? restore, or accept and reset forecast
            |     +-- spend flat, CPM up 20%+? -> auction season or competitor; accept lower volume or raise ceiling with client
            |     +-- spend flat, CPM flat, CTR/hook down, frequency up? -> creative fatigue -> new concepts (doctrine), not budget
            |     +-- one platform delivering less (learning, rejected ads, payment fail)? -> fix the account issue
            |
            +-- Brand search + direct down, paid sessions flat
            |     -> upstream demand fell: Meta reach to NEW people down (existing share up?), TikTok or creator
            |        push ended, PR spike last period. Fix create channel; do not raise brand bids.
            |
            +-- Organic non-brand down
                  -> GSC: ranking or page lost, algorithm update; SEO fix, not paid
```
- "Revenue is up" runs the same tree. The extra question: is it real growth (new customers up beyond noise) or a pull-forward (promo, email) or a credit shift? Do not scale on a pull-forward.

### Symptom lookup
| Symptom | Likely causes, in order | Number that confirms | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google brand conversions down, Google spend flat | 1. Meta reach to new people fell 2. creator or PR push ended 3. competitor took top spot | 1. Meta reach and new-audience share, 2 weeks earlier 2. calendar 3. auction insights, top-of-page rate | 1. Meta creative and budget 2. none 3. defend brand bid |
| Meta TW orders down, store revenue flat | email or brand search took last click; Meta still working | over-attribution ratio; TW first click share for Meta stable | no change |
| Direct sessions up sharply | untagged email or SMS links, creator links without UTMs, app traffic, offline | GA4 direct landing pages (product pages = untagged links); Klaviyo UTM settings | fix UTMs before crediting anything |
| Klaviyo revenue up 40%, store up 5% | discount campaigns and open-attribution claiming paid orders | Klaviyo click-only revenue; full-price order share; days after send | judge on store; cut discount frequency |
| New customers down, MER up | spend cut or Meta drifting to existing customers | Meta audience segments existing share; aMER | rebalance to new; show client aMER not MER |
| All platforms' CPA up the same day | site, stock, price, checkout, shipping change | GA4 CVR by device; Shopify stock; theme changelog | fix site; hold budgets |
| TikTok "not working" at day 10 | lag | blended new customers and brand search vs pre-TikTok; post-test window | hold to day 28 |
| Amazon sales up when Meta scaled | halo | Amazon brand sessions and sales by week vs Meta spend | count Amazon in Meta's iROAS |
| Organic brand clicks fell after brand campaign launched | cannibalisation | GSC brand clicks + Google brand clicks combined, flat | cut brand bids to defensive |

## 4. How each channel affects the others: before and after checklist

Before a change in channel X, write down (7-day and 28-day): store revenue, new-customer orders, aMER, MER, CM, Meta TW orders, Google brand and non-brand TW orders, GSC brand clicks and impressions, direct sessions, Klaviyo flow and campaign revenue (click-only), new sign-ups, Amazon sales if any, over-attribution ratio.

After: re-read the same list at 7, 14 and 28 days (and 35 for TikTok and YouTube). The channel's verdict is the change in store new customers and CM, attributed with the lag table, not its own dashboard.

| You change | Check in the other channels | Red flag |
|---|---|---|
| Meta budget | brand search, direct, sign-ups, flows, Amazon | Meta up 30%, brand search flat: spend reaching the wrong people |
| Meta creative mix | brand search and direct (memorable creative lifts them) | new winner with high Meta ROAS but no brand lift: may be retargeting-heavy |
| Google brand bids | GSC organic brand clicks, competitor share | total brand clicks down over 5% |
| PMax setup | brand campaign volume, new-customer share | PMax up, brand down by the same amount |
| Email send volume or discounts | Meta and Google TW orders on send days, full-price share | paid CPA "worse" on non-send days (pull forward) |
| TikTok budget | brand search, direct, Amazon, blended new customers | nothing moves after 4 weeks at 10%+ of social spend |
| Prices, offers, shipping thresholds | CVR on every channel | one channel blamed for a site-wide change |

### How to compute each check from Locus data
| Check | Data | Computation |
|---|---|---|
| Noise band | tw_daily orders | 2 / sqrt(orders in the 7-day window) |
| New vs returning split | tw_orders (customer order number) | order number 1 = new; compare 7-day sums vs 4-week same-weekday average |
| Over-attribution ratio | daily_insights (Meta purchase value), Google Ads value, Klaviyo attributed revenue, tw_daily revenue | sum of platform-claimed revenue / store revenue, weekly |
| Model spread | tw_ad_attr, models firstClick and lastPlatformClick | channel share of new-customer revenue under each model; spread = first minus last |
| Journey overlap | tw_orders journeys | share of channel X's orders with a different paid channel touch earlier in the journey |
| Demand memory | Search Console brand query rows, GA4 direct sessions, Klaviyo new profiles | 7-day sums vs prior 4 weeks and vs same weeks last year |
| Meta new vs existing | Meta audience segment breakdown | existing-customer share of spend and purchases, monthly |
| Cannibalisation of organic brand | Search Console brand clicks + Google brand campaign clicks | combined total before and after a brand bid change |
| Pull-forward | tw_daily orders | orders in the 3 to 7 days after a send or sale vs the same weekdays in the 4 weeks before |

### Channel roles by Mobius brand type (what the system looks like for each)
| Brand type | Repeat profile | Main create channel | Main harvest | Cross-channel watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Golf hard goods (wedges, putters, drivers, belts) | low repeat on clubs, some on accessories | Meta (creators, demos) | Google brand and Shopping on model names, email for launches | long consideration: brand and model-name searches lag Meta by 1 to 3 weeks; launches spike brand search; spring and Father's Day peaks |
| Golf apparel and accessories (socks, polos, gloves) | medium repeat, gifting | Meta | email, SMS | gifting peaks (Father's Day, Christmas) lift every channel at once; do not credit one channel |
| Party and wellness patches (consumable) | high repeat | Meta, TikTok, creators | email and SMS flows, subscriptions | returning revenue is large, so MER hides weak acquisition; always read aMER; festival calendar moves demand |
| Jewelry (Ice & Gold, Attentive for SMS) | gifting, medium repeat | Meta, creators | SMS (Attentive), brand search | SMS attribution windows differ from Klaviyo; Valentine's, Mother's Day, Christmas spikes |
| Pet, hockey gear | category intent plus seasonality (hockey season starts Sept) | Meta | Google Shopping non-brand, brand | category search is a bigger share; Google non-brand is a create-and-harvest channel here |
- Treat this as a starting map; confirm each brand's real split from TW first click vs last platform click before relying on it.

### Before proposing any cross-channel move, the Strategist answers
1. Which job does each channel involved do here: create, harvest, both, retain?
2. What is the store-level change, and is it beyond noise?
3. Which of the five real-vs-shift checks did I run, and what did they say?
4. What lag does the slowest affected channel have, and does my verdict respect it?
5. What else changed in the last 14 days (change log, sends, stock, price)?
6. What number would prove me wrong, and when will I look?

## 5. The CMO's weekly review (in this order, about 30 minutes per brand)
1. **Data health (2 min).** Data Health tab green? TW spend matches Meta and Google APIs? Any outage (Mobius Pulse)? If red, stop and fix.
2. **Money (5 min).** Last 7 days and MTD vs plan: store revenue, CM after ads, MER, aMER. Pacing ratio MTD (90 to 110% is on pace).
3. **New customers (5 min).** New-customer orders, NC-CAC vs ceiling, new vs returning split, 4-week trend. This is the health of the engine.
4. **Demand memory (3 min).** GSC brand clicks and impressions, direct sessions, sign-ups, week over week and vs last year. Falling memory with steady Meta spend = creative problem coming.
5. **Channels (8 min).** Per channel: spend, TW orders, CPA vs goal and keep line (doctrine), share of spend, marginal return if a spend step happened, over-attribution ratio. Meta: tests per doctrine, audience segment mix.
6. **Retention (3 min).** Flow revenue, campaign revenue (click-only), sends per week, deliverability, repeat rate.
7. **What we changed and what it did (2 min).** Each change from last week: expected vs actual on the list in section 4.
8. **Next week's moves (2 min).** At most 3 changes across all channels, each written in the suggestion format below. One change per channel.

## 5b. What a great suggestion looks like (worked cross-channel examples)
Format: WHAT to do. The NUMBER and its source. WHY in one line. WHAT TO WATCH and for how long. HOW SURE. Numbers below are illustrative unless marked as Mobius data.

Example 1: "Google is our best channel, move money there"
- Situation: Google brand TW last platform click ROAS 9.8x; Meta 1.9x. Client asks to move $3k a month from Meta to Google.
- WHAT: Keep Meta budget. Do not raise Google brand. Instead test PMax with brand excluded at its current budget.
- NUMBER: Over the last 8 weeks, weekly GSC brand clicks tracked Meta spend with a 1-week lag (brand clicks +22% the week after Meta's +30% step; -15% in the week after the September pause); auction insights show 1 competitor at 6% overlap; TW first click gives Meta 61% of new-customer orders, last platform click gives it 44%.
- WHY: Google brand closes the customers Meta finds; cutting Meta shrinks both.
- WATCH: brand clicks (paid + organic) and new customers weekly for 4 weeks after the PMax change.
- HOW SURE: high on direction (four checks agree), medium on size.

Example 2: revenue down 18% week on week (Lucky-sized brand, about 100 orders a week)
- Step 1 data: TW Meta spend matches the API. Step 2 noise: band is +/-20%, so -18% is borderline; last year's same week was -5%. Step 3 split: AOV flat, returning flat, new orders -27%. Step 4: sessions -21%, CVR flat. Step 5: Meta spend flat, CPM flat, frequency 2.9 vs 2.1 a month ago, CTR -24%, top 3 ads all older than 6 weeks. Step 6: brand clicks -12%, sign-ups -19%. Step 7: credit check clean. Step 8: no structure change; the last two weeks launched 2 tests instead of 5.
- WHAT: Launch the full 5 tests this week, 3 of them new concepts on the top angle, and replace (not just pause) the fatigued anchor in the main ad set.
- NUMBER: frequency 2.9 (Meta), CTR -24% (Meta), new orders -27% (TW), brand clicks -12% (GSC).
- WHY: Meta is reaching the same people more often, so fewer new people find the brand, and search and sign-ups fall behind it.
- WATCH: Meta CTR and frequency in 7 days, new-customer orders and brand clicks in 14 days.
- HOW SURE: medium-high; every leg of the tree points to creative, not budget or site.

Example 3: Klaviyo says email is up 45%
- WHAT: Report email at click-only revenue and move the weekly 20%-off send to every other week.
- NUMBER: Klaviyo-attributed revenue +45%; store revenue +4% (inside noise at this volume); 38% of Klaviyo-claimed orders also show a Meta click in TW order journeys; full-price share of orders fell from 71% to 58%; orders on the 3 days after each send are 14% below the 4-week baseline.
- WHY: the discount is moving full-price buyers onto discount days, not adding buyers.
- WATCH: store revenue, CM and full-price share over 4 weeks; revenue per recipient on non-discount sends.
- HOW SURE: medium; four weeks of alternating sends will settle it.

Example 4: client cut Meta 35% to protect margin
- WHAT: Restore Meta to 85% of the old budget in 20% steps every 3 days, and show the client aMER and CM dollars, not MER.
- NUMBER: Week 1 after the cut: MER 2.4x to 2.9x (Locus), revenue -6%. Week 3: brand clicks -18% (GSC), direct -14% (GA4), welcome-flow revenue -22% (Klaviyo), new customers -29% (TW), CM after ads -$3.1k a week vs before.
- WHY: the cut looked profitable for two weeks while built-up demand ran down; now the harvest channels are emptying.
- WATCH: new customers and brand clicks return within 14 days of restoring; CM per week back above the pre-cut level in 4 weeks.
- HOW SURE: medium-high; the lag pattern matches the table, nothing else changed.

Example 5: TikTok "failed" after 10 days
- WHAT: Keep TikTok at $60 a day (8% of social) to day 28, then judge on blended new customers and brand search, not TikTok-attributed orders.
- NUMBER: TikTok TW last platform click CPA $190 vs Meta $80; TW first click shows TikTok starting 9% of new-customer journeys; brand impressions +8% since launch (GSC).
- WHY: TikTok's lift arrives in the second half of a test and mostly through other channels (Haus).
- WATCH: blended new customers vs the 4 weeks before launch, brand impressions; stop at day 28 if neither moved beyond noise.
- HOW SURE: low until day 28; the test is designed to make it high.

Example 6: BFCM week, everything is up, where does the extra budget go
- Situation: day 3 of Black Friday week, store revenue +140% vs baseline, every platform reports record ROAS, client asks to double Meta for the weekend.
- WHAT: Raise Meta 20% today and 20% again in 3 days (doctrine cap), not 100%; hold Google brand at full impression share for the week; schedule the email and SMS sends as planned; no new tests during the week.
- NUMBER: Meta CPM +38% vs October (Meta); new-customer share of orders 41% vs 58% normal (TW); brand search impressions +190% (GSC); over-attribution ratio 1.9 vs 1.5 normal.
- WHY: this week's orders are mostly demand built in October and November being harvested; the extra Meta dollar buys at the year's highest CPM, and competitors bid on the brand during sales.
- WATCH: hourly revenue (TW single-day view), Meta CPA vs the season ceiling agreed with the client, brand impression share; then the 2-week post-sale dip and the December repeat rate of the BFCM cohort.
- HOW SURE: medium; BFCM is the week where every channel's own number is least trustworthy.

## 6. Traps
- "Harvest ROAS is high, so harvest is the best channel." It catches what others create. Funding it from create shrinks both.
- "We paused brand search and nothing happened, so brand search is useless." True only when uncontested; check competitors first, and it still captures the competitors' attempts.
- "Meta TW orders fell 20%, Meta is broken." Check total new customers. Email or brand search may simply have taken the last click.
- "Revenue fell the week after we raised Meta, so the raise failed." Look for a site, stock, or calendar cause; Meta raises do not lower revenue.
- "Cut spend, MER improved, success." MER rises on cuts; CM dollars and new customers decide.
- "Direct traffic is organic brand love." Direct is often untagged email, SMS and creator links. Check landing pages.
- "Every channel grew, look at the dashboards." Platforms add up to 130 to 180% of the store. Only store revenue grew or did not.
- "TikTok and YouTube are not incremental, their last-click CPA is 3x Meta's." Their effect arrives late and elsewhere.
- "One great week after a change proves the change." One week at under 400 orders is noise. Wait for the lag and two clean weeks.
- "The email list grew 30% because the popup is great." It grew because paid traffic grew; compare sign-ups per session.
- "Amazon has nothing to do with our Meta ads." For omnichannel brands, about a third of Meta's effect lands there.
- "Organic search fell, raise brand bids." Organic brand clicks are demand; organic non-brand is SEO. Neither is fixed by bids.
- "Two changes at once, both worked." Neither can be shown to have worked. One per channel per fortnight.
