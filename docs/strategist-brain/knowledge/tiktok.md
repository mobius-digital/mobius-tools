# TikTok ads: knowledge for the Strategist (October 2026)

Read with: `meta.md` (Meta is the core channel for every Mobius brand; TikTok is judged by what it adds on top) and the Mobius doctrine (Triple Whale last platform click is the default number; angle / concept / what we're testing is the creative framework).

What the Strategist can see for TikTok today: Triple Whale daily totals only (TikTok spend, TW-attributed orders and revenue under each model). Direct TikTok reporting (ad groups, ads, video metrics) is coming. Until it lands, every TikTok call must be made from TW totals plus the other channels: store revenue, MER, new-customer orders, Search Console brand queries, GA4 sessions by source, Meta CPA, Klaviyo signups. Say so in every TikTok suggestion.

## 1. How it works now (2025 to 2026)

### Ownership and platform risk
- US TikTok moved into TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC on Jan 22, 2026 (ByteDance 19.9%; Oracle, Silver Lake, MGX 15% each). Ads, Shop and creator tools kept running without a break.
- The US recommendation algorithm is being retrained on US data inside Oracle's cloud. Expect performance drift: creative that worked in 2025 can behave differently. Re-baseline CPMs and CPAs each quarter, not from 2025 benchmarks.
- eMarketer: US engagement rate rose from 6.6% to 6.9% after the deal; US ad revenue forecast +27.9% for 2026 (forecast, not results). More advertisers means CPMs will rise into Q4 2026.

### How delivery works
- TikTok is an interest graph, not a social graph. The For You feed decides in the first 1 to 2 seconds of watch whether to keep showing an ad. Early retention is the main delivery signal; an ad that loses viewers in second 1 gets cut regardless of targeting.
- The creative is the targeting, even more than on Meta. Broad targeting with strong native creative beats interest stacks.
- Fatigue is faster than Meta. Vendor data puts a winning ad's life at about 1 to 3 weeks at moderate spend and 3 to 5 days at $1,000+/day (not verified by TikTok). The early sign is the 2-second and 6-second view rate falling.

### Campaign types
- Smart+ (upgraded Oct 7, 2025): one buying flow with full, partial or manual automation, chosen module by module (targeting, budget, creative). TikTok says it will become the standard way to build campaigns. Symphony gen-AI tools sit inside it (recommended creatives, auto resize, music refresh, translate and dub).
- Smart+ needs creative volume: TikTok's guidance for Smart+ Web is at least 6 creative assets at setup (secondhand via Playad). Vendors report up to 30 asset groups per campaign (not verified).
- Smart+ is weak at exclusions (existing customers, recent buyers). If existing-customer spend must be controlled, use a manual Web Conversions campaign.
- Manual Web Conversions campaign with ad groups: better for diagnosis while the account is new; Smart+ better once there are 50+ conversions a week (practitioner consensus).
- Spark Ads: run an existing organic post (brand's or a creator's) as the ad. Keeps likes, comments, follows and the creator's handle. Creator gives a code for one post (7, 30, 60 or 365 days) or links the account. The caption is locked once authorised.
- Spark vs non-Spark: TikTok-reported +142% engagement, +43% conversion rate, -4% CPM (TikTok's own figures, directional). Vendor CPMs: in-feed $4 to $8, Spark $5 to $10.
- Search Ads (ads in TikTok search results) exist as a toggle or separate placement; useful only once a brand has search demand on TikTok itself.

### TikTok Shop and GMV Max
- Since July 2025 GMV Max is the only way to advertise TikTok Shop products (Product Shopping, Video Shopping and LIVE Shopping ads with Shop as the destination can no longer be created). Reported fully mandatory by Sep 2025.
- GMV Max: set a budget and an ROI target; TikTok picks creative (brand, creator, affiliate videos) and placements. Legacy campaigns migrated using their historical ROI as the target.
- GMV Max reporting counts paid, organic and affiliate Shop sales together. Its ROI is not comparable to a Web campaign's ROAS and overstates what the ads added.
- Creative Hub (Oct 2025): GMV Max reporting by campaign, product and creator.
- TikTok Shop US sales were about $15.8B in 2025 (+108%, eMarketer). Typical brand mix reported: Shopify 50 to 70% of revenue, TikTok Shop 5 to 15% (CFO benchmark, directional).
- If a brand's Shop orders sync into Shopify, they land in TW store revenue. Check the Shopify sales channel breakdown before reading MER after a Shop launch.

### Learning, budgets and bidding
- Learning exits at about 50 conversions per ad group in 7 days. An ad group under 20 conversions in its first 10 days rarely exits (secondhand). Edits to budget, bid, targeting or creative during learning restart it.
- Platform minimums: $50/day per campaign and $20/day per ad group (both must be met).
- Budget per ad group: TikTok's help article says daily budget of at least 10x target CPA for deep events like purchase; other sources say up to 50x for shallow events. Use 10x for purchase. At a $50 CPA that is $500/day per ad group, which most Mobius brands cannot fund: so use one ad group (or one Smart+ campaign) and accept learning limited.
- Bidding: Maximum delivery (default, spend-based) or Cost cap (goal-based). Value-based optimisation (highest value with an optional ROAS goal) exists for purchase campaigns (not verified in this research; check the account). Start on Maximum delivery.
- Cost cap budget-to-bid ratio of about 50:1 is quoted secondhand (not verified).

### Attribution
- Default for web: 7-day click, 1-day view. Options: click 1, 7, 14 or 28 days; view off, 1 or 7 days. TikTok recommends longer windows for considered products.
- Engaged view-through (EVTA): a view of 6+ seconds (or the full video if shorter) with no click, then a conversion; windows off, 1, 7 or 28 days.
- Assisted Conversions (Oct 2025): TikTok says more than 1 in 4 TikTok-attributed conversions happen when a person views an ad and buys on the site the same day through another channel.
- Third-Party Optimization with Google Analytics (Oct 2025): in early tests with 115 advertisers, conversions in GA rose 54% and CPA in GA fell 27% (TikTok-reported).
- Triple Whale last platform click needs a TikTok click in the journey. Most TikTok influence is a view followed by a search or a direct visit days later, so TW last platform click is the floor of TikTok's value, never the full value.

### What TikTok incrementality looks like (Haus, hundreds of tests, Dec 2025 report)
- Impact is back-loaded: lift grew 247% in the second half of tests and post-test window combined; the post-test window alone was 68% higher. Meta works in days; TikTok behaves like YouTube.
- Counting omnichannel instead of DTC only makes TikTok 1.9x as valuable: +34% extra sales on Amazon and +57% at physical retail, relative to DTC.
- 62% of TikTok's lift came from new customers.
- Who scales it: Gen Z-focused brands spend 2.7x more; AOV under $50 brands 2.8x more than AOV over $50; brands whose value shows in a short clip 1.5x more.
- Brands that kept testing settled at about 13% of search plus social spend (started 11%, peaked 15%). Brands turned TikTok off in about 14% of weeks.
- Long consideration case ($400+ product, month-long test): nearly all measured lift landed in the last 3 days (a promo). Short tests miss TikTok.

## 2. Decision rules

### Is TikTok worth budget for this brand? (gate, all five)
1. Meta is stable: trailing 4-week TW CPA at or within 15% of goal, and either Meta spend is $10k+/month or Meta is saturating (7-day frequency above 2.5, or new-customer CAC up 15%+ over the last quarter with flat creative output).
2. Creative supply: at least 10 native TikTok videos a month on top of Meta's needs (creators, ambassadors, founder, staff). Without this, every dollar is better spent on more Meta concepts.
3. Product fit: the value shows in under 10 seconds of video (a demo, a reaction, a before/after). AOV under about $100 is easier; over $150 needs a 4 to 8 week view and Google brand search to catch the buyers.
4. Margin to carry a test: the brand can fund 6 weeks at $100+/day with TW-attributed TikTok ROAS 30 to 60% below Meta without breaking its MER floor.
5. Tracking: TikTok pixel plus Events API live (Shopify TikTok app), TikTok connected in Triple Whale, UTMs on every ad.
- Two or more fail: do not launch. Say why and what would change the answer.

### Fit by Mobius category (hypotheses to test, not facts)
- Party and wellness patches: strongest fit (low AOV, young buyers, visual demo, gifting). Check TikTok's health and wellness ad policy before claims; TikTok Shop category approval may be needed.
- Jewelry (Ice & Gold): strong fit (visual, gifting, creator try-on). Q4 and Valentine's peaks.
- Golf equipment and apparel: medium. Golf TikTok is large, but buyers skew older and AOV is $60 to $230. Best entry is Spark Ads on creator and ambassador golf content, not brand-made ads.
- Pet: medium to strong (pet content is native; demo works). Hockey gear: medium (youth players plus parents as buyers; seasonal).

### Test design (first TikTok run for a brand)
- Length: 6 weeks minimum. Do not judge before week 4. Haus shows most lift arrives late.
- Budget: $100 to $200/day ($3k to $6k a month). Under $50/day the platform minimum alone eats the test and nothing is readable.
- Structure: one campaign. Either Smart+ Web with 6 to 10 distinct videos, or one manual ad group (broad, Maximum delivery) with 3 to 5 videos. No interest stacking, no split by product.
- Optimisation event: Complete Payment if the budget is at least 3x target CPA per day; otherwise Initiate Checkout for the first 14 days, then a new ad group on Complete Payment (switching the event in place restarts learning anyway).
- Creative: 60%+ Spark Ads from creators or ambassadors, the rest brand-made native edits. Never Meta studio ads with a logo intro.
- Refresh: 3 to 5 new videos every 7 to 14 days. TikTok fatigues 2 to 3 times faster than Meta.
- Hold everything else steady: no Meta budget step over 20%, no new sale or price change in the first 3 weeks if avoidable. Otherwise the read is mixed with other changes.

### How to read the test (Strategist can do this from TW plus the other channels)
- Baseline: the 4 weeks before launch for store revenue, new-customer orders, MER, Search Console brand impressions and clicks, GA4 direct and organic sessions, Meta TW CPA.
- Pass if, in weeks 3 to 6 vs baseline (adjusted for any season shift seen in prior years): new-customer orders up by at least 50% of (TikTok spend / goal new-customer CAC), MER at or above floor, and brand search impressions up 10%+.
- Credit range for TikTok: floor = TW last platform click; middle = TikTok-reported 7-day click without view; ceiling = TikTok-reported with 1-day view and EVTA. Record the ratio of ceiling to floor in the first 6 weeks and use it as the brand's TikTok multiplier until a holdout replaces it.
- Best evidence: a geo holdout (TikTok on in half of states matched by past sales, off in the other half, 4 weeks plus 2 weeks after). Run it once TikTok spend passes $10k a month.
- Fail: after 6 weeks new customers did not rise, brand search was flat and MER fell. Stop paid spend; keep Spark-boosting the best organic or creator posts at $20 to $30/day only if they pull brand search.

### Scale rules
- Raise budget 20 to 30% every 3 to 4 days when the TW floor CPA holds within 20% of week-3-to-6 levels and MER holds.
- Each budget step needs fresh creative behind it: at $300+/day plan 5 to 8 new videos a week.
- Healthy share once proven: 10 to 20% of paid social spend (Haus brands settled at about 13% of search plus social). Above 25% for a non-Gen-Z brand needs a holdout to justify it.
- Move to Smart+ (or more automation modules) once the account makes 50+ purchases a week; before that, manual is easier to diagnose.

### Budget sizing by stage

| Stage | Daily budget | Structure | New videos | Judged on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not ready (gate fails) | $0, or $20 to 30 Spark boost of proven organic posts | Spark only | None required | Brand search only |
| First test | $100 to 200 | 1 campaign, 1 ad group or Smart+ Web | 3 to 5 every 7 to 14 days | Incrementality read, weeks 3 to 6 |
| Proven | 10 to 20% of paid social | 1 to 2 campaigns, Smart+ once 50+ purchases a week | 5 to 8 a week at $300+/day | TW floor CPA with the brand's multiplier, MER |
| Seasonal push (BFCM, gifting) | Ramp 3 to 4 weeks before the peak | Same campaign, no new structure | Double the cadence | New customers during the peak vs last year |

- Start TikTok ramps for Q4 in early October, not Black Friday week: TikTok's lift builds over weeks (Haus case: nearly all lift in the last 3 days of a month-long test, at the promo).

### Cut rules
- Pause an ad whose 2-second view rate fell 30%+ from its first 3 days, or that spent 2x target CPA with no click-through purchases and weak view rates.
- Cut budget 20 to 30% (not off) if the TW floor CPA is 2x the test average for 2 weeks and brand search fell. Going fully dark loses the account's learning; restarting costs another 2 to 3 weeks.
- Do not judge a TikTok ad on TW last platform click alone. A TikTok ad with zero TW sales can still be the reason Meta and Google got theirs.

### Creative native rules (what makes a TikTok ad work)
- 9:16, full screen, sound on, people on camera, filmed on a phone. Polish lowers watch time.
- Hook in the first 1 to 2 seconds: a face, a movement, a claim or a question on screen. TikTok's own guidance: hook within 6 seconds, state the proposition in the first 3.
- Show the product working by second 5. Text at 5 to 10 words per second, inside the safe zone (no text in the bottom 20% or the right-side buttons).
- Length 15 to 35 seconds for most DTC products; longer only for demos with a story.
- Sounds: only the Commercial Music Library for ads. Trending sounds that are not cleared get ads muted or rejected.
- Angle stays the same across Meta and TikTok; the concept changes. "Golfer reacts to his first flop shot with the wedge" is TikTok-native; a studio product rotation is not.
- Spark first for creators. Ask for codes of 60 days or more so a winner is not cut off mid-run.
- Creative variations that count on TikTok: new first 2 seconds, new creator, new setting. Same rule as Meta's Andromeda: copy changes alone are not a new ad.

### Spark Ads and creator operations
- Every creator deal includes Spark rights for 60 to 90 days with an option to extend, and a clause that the post stays public and unedited while authorised.
- Before entering a code: the post is public, the caption is final (locked after authorisation), the code covers the full planned run.
- Boost a creator's organic post only when its organic numbers are above the creator's own median (views and shares). Organic traction is a free pre-test.
- Keep a sheet of codes with expiry dates. A winner that expires mid-run drops delivery overnight and looks like fatigue.
- Ambassador and creator videos made for Meta partnership ads often work as Spark Ads with no re-edit. Reuse the creator's own TikTok post, not the Meta file.

### Tracking rules
- TikTok pixel plus Events API through the Shopify TikTok app, with email and phone passed (hashed) on purchase. Check TikTok Events Manager for purchase events vs Shopify orders: a gap over 30% is a tracking problem first.
- UTMs on every ad (utm_source=tiktok, utm_medium=paid, campaign and ad ids) so GA4 and TW can separate paid from organic TikTok.
- Set the reporting attribution in TikTok to 7-day click, 1-day view for comparability with Meta. Read EVTA separately, as the ceiling.
- Add "How did you hear about us?" (post-purchase survey) to the order confirmation if the brand runs TikTok at $3k+/month. TikTok answers vs TW-attributed TikTok orders is a cheap multiplier check.

### Metrics to read once direct TikTok reporting lands
- 2-second view rate (hook): compare each ad to the account's own top third, not to a web benchmark. A falling 2-second rate is the first fatigue signal.
- 6-second view rate and average watch time (hold): an ad with a good hook and poor 6-second rate has a weak middle; re-edit the body.
- CTR (destination clicks / impressions) and CVR on the site (TW or GA4): high CTR with low CVR is a page or offer mismatch, same as Meta.
- CPM: rising on the same ads while view rates fall is fatigue; rising on all ads at once is the auction.
- Judge ads by spend share plus view rates while TW last-click sales are few. Judge the campaign by the incrementality read in this file.

### Weekly read order (TikTok)
1. Tracking: TikTok purchase events vs Shopify orders; GA4 sessions with source tiktok.
2. Store truth: TW revenue, new-customer orders, MER vs the pre-TikTok baseline.
3. Halo: Search Console brand impressions and clicks, Google brand conversions, Meta CPA and new-customer share.
4. TikTok totals in TW: spend, floor ROAS, and the ceiling-to-floor multiplier.
5. Creative: days since the last new video; ads past 14 days live; codes expiring in the next 14 days.
6. Shop (if live): Shopify orders by sales channel; DTC new customers vs Shop orders.

### TikTok Shop / GMV Max rules
- Launch Shop only if the brand will run it as a channel: fulfilment inside TikTok's SLA, an affiliate program with samples, someone owning it weekly. Ads alone do not make Shop work.
- Set the GMV Max ROI target from margin: break-even ROI = 1 / contribution margin (after Shop fees and affiliate commission). Start at 1.2x break-even; lower it 10% at a time if spend will not deliver.
- Judge Shop on total brand revenue: DTC new customers and Amazon (if any) must not fall more than Shop adds. Shop pulling buyers off the website at a 6 to 8% fee plus affiliate commission is not growth.
- Affiliate success = number of active selling creators and their sell-through, not the number of creators signed.

## 4. How TikTok affects the other channels

- TikTok mostly creates demand that other channels collect. Expect, with a 1 to 3 week lag after TikTok spend rises:
  - Search Console: brand-query impressions and clicks up, often with new product-name queries.
  - Google Ads: brand campaign conversions up; Shopping clicks on the hero SKU up.
  - GA4: direct and organic sessions up; sessions with source tiktok are a small share of the effect.
  - Meta: possibly lower CPA on the same products (warmed buyers), and Meta view-through claiming TikTok-primed buyers.
  - Amazon or retail where the brand sells there (Haus: +34% Amazon, +57% retail relative to DTC).
  - Klaviyo: popup signups up; welcome-flow revenue up.
- Credit shifting: when TikTok spend rises, Meta, Google brand and Klaviyo will all show "better" numbers that are partly TikTok's. When TikTok is cut, they will look worse 2 to 4 weeks later. Do not credit Meta for a lift that started the week TikTok launched.
- Real growth vs credit shifting: store revenue and new-customer orders (TW) must rise; MER must hold. If the platforms together claim more new orders than the store gained, the extra is double counting.
- Check BEFORE a TikTok launch or budget change: the 4-week baseline (store revenue, new customers, MER, brand search impressions, GA4 direct sessions, Meta TW CPA); the Meta change log for anything else changing that week; the Klaviyo calendar; planned sales or price changes; stock on the products TikTok will push.
- Check AFTER (weekly for 6 weeks, then monthly): the same list, plus the ratio of TikTok-reported to TW-attributed conversions (the multiplier), and Shopify orders by sales channel if Shop is live.
- Funding rule: fund a TikTok test from new budget or from Meta's least efficient spend, never by cutting Meta below the level that keeps its best ad sets delivering. Otherwise the test reads as "TikTok hurt Meta".

## 5. What a great suggestion looks like

Format: WHAT to do. NUMBER and its source. WHY in one line. WHAT TO WATCH and for how long. HOW SURE. (Numbers below are illustrative.)

**Example 1: say no, with the reason**
- WHAT: Do not start TikTok for this golf brand this quarter. Put the planned $3,000/month into 2 more Meta concept tests a week instead.
- NUMBER: Meta TW CPA $80 vs $52 goal over 30 days (Locus Meta + TW); 7-day frequency 1.6; 4 new ads launched in the last 3 weeks; no TikTok-native creative in the library (Asana test library).
- WHY: Meta is not saturated and is starved of creative; TikTok would compete for the same thin creative supply.
- WATCH: Re-check the gate monthly. Revisit when Meta CPA is within 15% of goal for 4 weeks and the ambassador program delivers 10+ usable videos a month.
- HOW SURE: High. Three of five gate checks fail.

**Example 2: launch a clean test**
- WHAT: Launch one Smart+ Web campaign for the patch brand at $150/day for 6 weeks: 8 videos (5 creator Spark posts, 3 brand edits), optimise for Complete Payment, broad, Maximum delivery. Hold Meta budget steps at 20% or less during weeks 1 to 3.
- NUMBER: Meta TW CPA $24 vs $28 goal for 5 weeks; 7-day frequency 2.9 and rising; new-customer CAC up 18% this quarter (TW); AOV $38; 14 creator videos approved last month.
- WHY: Meta is saturating its audience at a low AOV with young buyers, which is the profile Haus found TikTok scales best for.
- WATCH: Weeks 3 to 6 vs the 4-week baseline: at least 75 more new-customer orders than baseline across weeks 3 to 6 (50% of the $4,200 spent in those weeks divided by the $28 goal), Search Console brand impressions up 10%+, MER at or above floor. Do not judge before week 4.
- HOW SURE: Medium. Fit is strong; the unknown is the multiplier between TikTok's own numbers and the TW floor, which this test will measure.

**Example 3: protect a TikTok test from a bad cut**
- WHAT: Keep TikTok at $150/day for 3 more weeks; refresh 4 videos this week. Do not cut on TW-attributed ROAS.
- NUMBER: TikTok TW last-platform-click ROAS 0.7 in week 3 (TW); Search Console brand impressions +22% vs baseline starting week 2; Google brand conversions +17%; new-customer orders +11% with Meta spend flat (TW, Google Ads, Search Console).
- WHY: The lift is showing up in search and new customers, which is where TikTok's value lands; TW last click is only the floor.
- WATCH: Week 4 to 6 brand impressions and new-customer orders; if both fall back to baseline after the refresh, cut to $50/day Spark-only.
- HOW SURE: Medium. Three channels moved together after launch with nothing else changed, but only a holdout would prove it.

## 6. Traps

- "TikTok ROAS in Triple Whale is 0.6, kill it." TW last platform click needs a TikTok click; most TikTok buyers view, then search. Read brand search, new customers and MER first.
- "TikTok Ads Manager says 3x ROAS." With 1-day view and EVTA it counts people who would have bought anyway. Use it as the ceiling, not the answer.
- "Two weeks is enough to know." Haus: most lift arrives in the second half of a test and after it. Judge at week 4 to 6.
- "Reuse the Meta winners." Studio Meta ads with logo intros lose viewers in second 1. Same angle, new native concept.
- "GMV Max ROI 4x means ads made 4x." It includes organic and affiliate Shop sales. And Shop sales may be website sales that moved.
- "Meta got better after TikTok launched, so Meta's new creative is working." Check timing. A brand-search and new-customer lift that starts with TikTok is TikTok's.
- "More ad groups = more tests." At $20/day each none of them learn. One ad group or one Smart+ campaign until 50 purchases a week.
- "Use the trending sound." Uncleared music gets ads muted or rejected. Commercial Music Library only.
- "A creator flopped, so creators do not work here." Judge creator Spark Ads by batch of 5+, not one video.
- "2025 benchmarks still apply." The US algorithm is being retrained since the Jan 2026 joint venture. Re-baseline each quarter.
- "Fund TikTok by cutting Meta." Cutting Meta below its working level makes both look worse and muddies the read.
- "TikTok sessions in GA4 are tiny, so TikTok is not driving traffic." Most TikTok-driven visits arrive as direct, organic or brand search days later.
- "The halo is proven because TikTok says so." Assisted Conversions and vendor halo models are TikTok's or a vendor's view. Only a holdout or a clean before/after with nothing else changed counts as evidence.
- "We tested TikTok in 2024 and it failed." Smart+, GMV Max, the attribution tools and the US algorithm have all changed since. A 2024 result is a weak prior, not an answer.
