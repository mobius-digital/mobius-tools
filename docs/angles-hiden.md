## 1. The brand (hiden.com, checked 2026-10-02)

HIDEN is a live technical hunting brand: "Silent. Comfortable. Deadly." and "Hide in the wide open".
58 products, about $20 to $160 (Infernal Silent Hunt Jacket $159.95, Super Stalk pant $139.95,
Reaper hoodie $59.95, hats $24.95 to $29.95). Two own camo patterns, Arid Exile (desert) and
Backwoods Exile (forest). "Over 100 five star reviews", outfitter testimonials. No reversible
products. Instagram @hiden_usa. Hiden, LLC (Miami) holds several Class 25 trademark filings.

The relaunch (Chris's mockups and the password-protected demo store, which we could not read):
reversible lifestyle apparel, black one side, camo or the HIDEN print the other. Lines:
"Everyone has a little country hiden inside", "Normal for now. Country whenever.", "Just flip it.",
"Two worlds. One you.", "From the office to the outdoors". Products shown: men's and women's
reversible puffers, hoodies, vests; a women's "Haute and Hiden" collection (jumpsuit, sweat set,
monogram-print bags and loungewear); hats, tumblers. Hidden details Chris named: a swatch to clean
sunglasses, a chapstick pocket, a HIDEN keychain koozie, a QR code on the tag that plays how the
piece was made (cotton farm to final touch). "Our Story" page promises sustainable sourcing,
ethical partnerships and "Good clothes do good things".

### Claims to confirm with Chris before creators use them
Prices (none set). Which camo goes on the reversible side (HIDEN's Exile patterns or the new

The Haute and Hiden print needs a trademark review (it reads close to the LV monogram) before any

## 4. Voice of customer (forums, reviews, press; Reddit and TikTok were blocked)

- Partners police where camo goes: a WeddingWire thread negotiates a groom from full camo down to
  pocket squares and socks. Strongest support for "normal for now, country whenever".
- Hunters want clothes that work in town and resent the camo price premium (HuntTalk).
- Reversible buyers love "2 jackets in 1" and travel, then mostly wear one side; complaints are
  shallow pockets, bulk, one-way zippers, a side that is not their style (Rent the Runway, KUIU).
- Outdoor-brand gift reviews are written by wives, girlfriends and mums; success = his grin and how
  often he wears it (Legendary Whitetails).
- Women resent "shrink it and pink it": boxy men's cuts, worse build for the price (Tradbow, Savage Arms).
- Camo reads as politically coded to part of the audience (GQ). Keep the voice warm, no politics.

## 7. To run the seed

1. Add HIDEN to Locus (Settings > + New client, or + Add a brand once its Meta account is shared).
2. `set HIDEN_ACT=act_...` and `set LUCIDE_NODES=<path to lucide-static@1.46.0/icon-nodes.json>`.
3. `python migrations/seed_hiden.py` in profit/worker, then
   `npx wrangler d1 execute mobius-account-health --remote --file migrations/seed_hiden.sql` ONCE.
4. Add 'hiden' to SLUGS in angles/build.py and run `python angles/build.py` when Cole switches it live.
5. On the Ambassadors tab: paste the TRYBE campaign URL and the logo URL, review, switch on.
