Make: ideas to live ads on one board

Pipeline is the first tab, with the stage names your Asana boards already use (Creative Briefs, Creative Studio, Ready to Launch, Launched and testing, Analyze results) so nothing is renamed on the team. Reports: the week as a story with the ads in it

Four numbers with one comparison each, the human sentence, then three lists fed by the system (This week from the Monday call, Done from Tasks, From you from Requests), then the ads as cards that open the ad panel. The client's copy is this page. Agency view is a row of report cards: seven waiting to send, each with its one-line state.

Work: the Monday call is a runner, not a form

Answers What do we decide about Lucky this week, who does what by when, and what will Finish write where?

Steps across the top with counts, one brand per step. Inside a tab: where features and settings live

Your question was: a tab sometimes needs its own features and its own settings, so are those sub-tabs, panels, or a general settings page? The rule below decides it every time, so nothing gets invented per screen.

1. Views of the same list are tabs

Calls, Accounts, Ad wall, Tests, Changes are all the ad account seen a different way, so they are tabs under the title. A tab never has tabs; a second level is a segmented control in the control bar (Meta | Google on Accounts, List | Board on Pipeline).

2. Things you do to the page are header actions

One primary button, one quiet button, and "..." for the rest. Do all safe calls, + Brief, Send to client. Never in the top bar, never floating.

3. Things you do to one item are in its panel

Pause, lower budget, make it a task, put it on the call, push back, open in Ads Manager. The panel is the same component for an ad, a task, a leak or a post, so the buttons are always in the same place.

4. Settings that change what you see stay on the page

Period, compare, attribution, sort, size, colour, columns, which brands: control pills on the page, remembered per page. Social's "which brands to watch" is a pill on Social, not a Settings page.

5. Settings that change how Locus judges live in the page's "..."

The $64 goal CPA, the cut line, the judging spend, the posting credits, Slack channel for the brief: a sheet opened from the page's "..." menu ("Ads rules" from Calls, "Report settings" from Reports). The same sheet is reachable from Settings, so there is one place to edit and two doors.

6. Only connections and people live in Settings

Integrations, logins, who sees what, brand name and logo, the client's edition. These change once. Everything else has a door on the page it affects.

Examples from the mocks

  • Social needs a brand watchlist, a credits balance and a posting schedule. Watchlist: a pill on the Social tab ("Takomo, Vice, Bad Birdie + 3"). Credits: one line in the page's "..." sheet and the 5am card, never in the content. Posting: Week is a view of the same tab, the composer is a sheet.
  • Ads needs the goal CPA and the cut line. Shown as a caption wherever a call is judged ("goal $64, cut at 1.5x after 3x spent"); edited from "..." then Ads rules on Calls, the same sheet Settings opens.
  • Pipeline needs stage names, owners and the Launch to Meta mapping. Stages and owners are edited on the board (rename a column, owner pill). Launch to Meta is a header action with its own confirm card that lists exactly what it will create.
  • Reports need a send day, channel and what the client sees. "..." then Report settings on Reports. Preview as the client is a tab, not a setting.

Ad, ad set, call, leak, task, brief, post, report, request, calendar date. Build plan: seven phases, each one shippable

Every phase leaves Locus working for everyone, with every old page id still routing. The new shell goes first so each later phase lands inside it instead of beside it. No worker changes until phase 5; everything before it is the front end reading what the workers already return.
