Locus v4 review, round one

Cole walked the live app as Lucky Golf on 21 October 2026, right after v4 went on by default. This page holds every note he made, the decision on each, and the order the work ships in. The next session builds from here.

The one sentence. The organisation is right. The visuals are not premium yet: every chart, ring, bar and sparkline reads as a sketch of the idea, not a finished instrument, and too many cards tell you something without letting you click into the numbers behind it. Fix the instruments once, in the kit, and every page gets it.

Answers to the questions you asked

What do premium SaaS products do with bars and rings? Linear, Stripe, Vercel, Triple Whale and Northbeam share the same habits: thin strokes and small radii (2 to 4px, never a pill), one accent colour with the rest in two or three greys, numbers set in the chart not under it, a visible scale (a baseline, a target line, an axis label), a legend on one line or on hover, and motion on first paint (a ring that fills, a bar that grows over 400ms). Gradients are used only as a soft fill under a line, never on a solid bar. Pastel chunky blocks read as Canva. So: the river becomes a thin segmented bar on a baseline with its legend on one line and the numbers inside the segments where they fit; rings get a thin track, a thin progress stroke, the number inside and a fill animation; sparklines get real height, a baseline and a min and max; the dial, waterfall and funnel follow the same rules.
The brown on the October ring. It is the amber "close to plan" status colour drawn as a wide stroke, which turns muddy at that width. Status goes to a small chip; the ring itself is always the brand blue.
Definitions under the river, or hover? Both, smaller: one legend line with a dot, a word and a number per segment, and the sentence on hover. The row of three cards moves up by what that saves. "A normal day keeps about $325" sits next to the headline number, not under everything.
Should the character's sentence be clickable? Yes. It opens the Ask thread with that sentence as the first message, so "why 12 things to change" is one click.
Needs you with one card. When it has one item it folds into the strip under the heading (one line, one button). The card only appears when there are two or more, and it scrolls inside itself.
Does Spent mean ad spend? Yes. The word becomes "Ad spend" everywhere and "Banked" becomes "Revenue". Fixed costs never sit in that strip; they live on the P&L.
The customer card's words. Plain ones: "What a new customer costs", "Their first order", "Left after costs", "Come back and order again". The dial and its rule line go, replaced by one sentence in English ("Each $1 of ads brings $1.03 back in 90 days. Under $1.50 the first order does not pay for the customer.") and a bar with the 1.5x and 3x marks labelled.
Switching attribution on attributed numbers. Agreed as a general rule: wherever a number is credited (first orders by channel, ROAS, channel revenue) the model menu is on that card, not only in the top bar: platform, Triple Whale last click, first click, linear, and Triple Attribution where the brand has it.
Email and stock under Money? No. The rail becomes: Today, Money (Month, P&L, Customers, Store, Site), Marketing (Calls, Accounts, Ads, Tests, Changes, Email, Search), Products (Stock, Buying, Drops), Make, Calendar, Reports, Work. One extra item, every page where you would look for it.
Stock per wedge, per size. Two views on Stock: by product (what Shopify calls the product) and by variant (loft and hand for a wedge, size for a polo), with the variant view the default for brands that sell variants as products. The table fits the screen; the explainer paragraph goes into Help.
CPA says cut, ROAS says fine. Top of funnel. Hook rate and hold rate. You are right that more rules make it dumber. The change is in what the row shows, not in more rules: each call opens the whole picture of the ad set (cost per sale against goal, ROAS against goal, AOV, spend share, CPM, CTR, frequency, new customer share, and for videos hook, hold and watch through) with the call written as a suggestion and the reason naming which numbers drove it and which argue against it. Where ROAS and CPA disagree the row says so and makes no call. A set with low ROAS but a high new-customer share and high CTR is flagged "feeds the funnel" instead of Cut. Creative notes (strong hook, weak hold; high CTR, low spend) become a line on the ad, pointing at an iteration, not an instruction. The media buyer decides; Locus lays out the evidence.
TikTok in Changes. Yes, the day a brand connects it.
Why does a rail item reopen the last tab? It remembers where you were in that place. It stays, and the rail gets a collapse button.
Dashboards showing Bonk under Lucky. A bug: the list ignores the brand picker. Fixed in the first pass.
Inspiration: how do I save one? Today only from the Social feed and the ad library inside Locus. Adding: a Save to Locus share target (paste a link or an image into the Ask bar, or share from the phone), and a bookmarklet for the desktop. Inspiration then shows all angles, not only the season set.

Build order

  1. Pass A, the instrument kit (lx.js, lx.css, v4.js, v2.css): river, ring, sparkline, funnel, bar list, waterfall, dial-to-bar, table standard, tile detail panel. Built once, used by every page. The biggest change in how the app feels.
  2. Pass B, words, layout and panels page by page, in the order you walked: Today, Brief, Month, P&L, Customers, Store and the rest of Money, Calls rows, the ad tables, Pipeline cards, Season, Reports, Dashboards bug, Requests line.
  3. Pass C, structure: Marketing and Products in the rail, Make out of the top bar, Test calls into Tests, Tools renamed, rail collapse, Save to Locus.
  4. Pass Q, the ad set picture: the whole picture per set, for and against, conflicts make no call, funnel flag, creative notes. Designed with you before it ships, because it changes what the team reads every morning.
  5. Then Studio's own pass and Stock variants.
