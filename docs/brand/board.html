Locus, the dot

3 directions

A locus is a point moving along a path. The helper is the dot: it lives in the ask bar, looks aside while it thinks, lights up when it has a call for you, and goes dim when all is well. All three directions keep that one character and differ in what the path is.

Beside the Mobius mark

The Mobius mark leaves the apps and stays on the agency site. The new marks should sit beside it like relatives, not copies: the same confident weight, a different shape. In ink first, then in colour.

Mobius is a ribbon; Locus and Kept are rings. The ribbon's band is about 14% of its height, the rings' stroke is 8% of the box and 12% of the ring, so the three share a weight without sharing a shape. Each ring sits on a 64 grid, radius 21, stroke 5, round caps, currentColor.

The accent word

One serif italic word per page head, the word that carries the sentence. Instrument Serif is already loaded by every Mobius tool and sits well against Inter: a narrow, high-contrast italic next to a plain sans. The rule is in logo/kit.css as the class kit-accent.

The rule
  • One accent word per page head. Never two, never a whole sentence.
  • The word that carries the meaning: actually, change, kept, paused. Not the product name.
  • Instrument Serif italic, regular weight, 8% larger than the sans so the x-heights line up.
  • On a surface the word takes the product's colour. On a field it stays white like the rest.
  • Numbers, buttons and labels never take the serif. It is a voice, not a style.
