# BFCM 2026 ad templates (shared, every brand)

Written Oct 13, 2026. Twelve layouts that every Mobius brand builds its Black Friday / Cyber Monday batches from.
Brand chats pick the template, fill the slots with their own offer, product and voice, and make the ads in Locus Studio.
Batches, ad counts, offers and dates live in `docs/bfcm-ads-gameplan-2026-10-13.md`; this file is only the layouts.

## Where this comes from

- **Our own BFCM 2025** (Nov 1 to Dec 2, 2025). Orders = Triple Whale lastPlatformClick (read from the Locus TW records,
  because the TW MCP is plan-blocked on 5 of 7 stores), spend = Meta. Judged ad set first: anchor = 50%+ of its set's spend.
  Brand averages: Lucky $137 CPA / 1.08 ROAS, Dartee $63.5 / 1.54, Party Patch $142 / 0.43.
  **Gaps:** Grunk has TW orders but no spend (Meta account not readable, ad names missing), so no winners named;
  Bonk's pixel was not live until Dec 8, 2025; Ice & Gold attribution starts Dec 2, 2025; Yak is not in Triple Whale.
- **Creative templates step 1** (Oct 9, 96 TW winners, May to Oct 2026): statics beat video on 6 of 7 brands; hero + one
  line, feature callouts, scene + bullets, price ladder, moment wrapper, review over product.
- **Cole's 17 formats** (`profit/season.js` `FORMATS`, Mei's TikTok). The ones that earned a template are folded in below;
  the rest are hooks or copy lines, listed at the end.
- **The Atria swipe boards** (Season `SWIPE` boards, BFCM / Brands / *, Themes / Gifting and Bundles): 16 boards, about 138
  unique ads, about 70 real BFCM offers. Atria answered every call (rate limits, **no credit errors** on Oct 13).
  The phase boards are nearly empty (Early BFCM 5, Sign Up 4, Black Friday weekend 3, Gift cards / Order by Christmas /
  Boxing Day / Valentine's 0); the per-brand boards hold the same 12 to 14 golf BF ads filed many times.
- **Outside research**: Atria library + web roundups, 41 reference ads from BFCM 2024 and 2025. The Meta Ad Library tool
  only returns current ads, so old ads were found through Atria (an Atria id minus the "m" is the Meta id:
  `facebook.com/ads/library/?id=<id>`). Days running is the only winner signal for other brands' ads.

## What our own data says (read this before picking)

1. **The offer-first banner won on every brand that has data.** Lucky BFCM Static #1 ($83 CPA vs $137 average), Dartee
   BFCM #2 Still ($55 vs $63.5), Party Patch BFCM #15 Still ($36 vs $142, small). Best with a real product photo under the offer.
2. **Early access 7 to 10 days before Friday was each brand's cheapest BFCM set.** Lucky Early BF $88 CPA (Static #10
   $55); Dartee Early BF Still C "SMART GOLFERS SHOP EARLY" $48.
3. **Evergreen winners kept winning through BFCM with no offer at all.** Lucky Mallet Putter A ($99, anchor 89%),
   Dartee 238 A creator video (the best ad of the month, $50, 74 orders), Party Patch 312-9 UGC ($86).
   Never switch the evergreen anchors off for the weekend; add the offer to them (template 2).
4. **What lost:**
   - **Leading with the free gift.** Dartee "FREE HAT? Only during Early BFCM" (hat shown, no belt) $80 CPA. The product leads, the gift is the second line.
   - **One product with a smaller discount and feature callouts.** Lucky Static #11 "Long iron killer, 30% off" spent the most in its set at $296 CPA.
   - **Discount UGC spread thin.** Lucky BFCM UGC set $149, Dartee BFCM #15 UGC $106, Party Patch BFCM #21 UGC 1 order. Video at BFCM = creators and Ravo, judged by numbers, not a template.
   - **Holiday-themed angles.** Party Patch Thanksgiving and Friendsgiving sets, about $1K, almost no orders.
   - **Not run at all in 2025:** bundle, value stack, gift guide, countdown, review, native phone. Templates 5, 6 and 7 are tests for us.

## The twelve templates

Studio routes used below:
- **Use as the ad**: a real photo kept as shot, Studio only adds the words. **Lucky always uses this, or a layout around
  real cutouts. The model never draws a Lucky product.**
- **AI make**: Studio draws the whole ad (other brands only, with the product accuracy checks).
- **Canva master**: any ad with exact prices, values or tiers. Studio may make the background; the numbers are typed by a
  person in a locked layout per brand.
- **Make it look like this**: up to 2 reference ads per line. The references named under each template are the ones to pull.

Every template is 4:5 with everything inside the centre square; the 9:16 is Studio's re-layout. No em dashes in any slot.
Never name a competitor in ad copy.

### 1. Offer-first banner
**Phase:** early bird ("came early"), the weekend, Cyber Monday (sticker swap).
**Layout:** the offer is the biggest thing on the frame and takes the top half. Real product photo below or behind it,
big and lit. Black, or the brand's loudest colour; gold on black for Lucky. 5 to 12 words on the image in total.
**Slots:**
- `OFFER` the number or the deal, biggest type ("40% OFF SITEWIDE", "BUY ONE, GET ONE", "$100 OFF 3 CLUBS")
- `EVENT` small, above or below the offer ("BLACK FRIDAY", "BLACK FRIDAY CAME EARLY", "EARLY ACCESS")
- `FRICTION` one line that removes a step: "NO CODE NEEDED", "SHIPS FREE"
- `END` optional: "ENDS MONDAY"
- `PRODUCT` the hero product or a tight row of 3 to 5
- Headline (below the ad): a short line in the brand's voice ("Gold Friday Starts Now")
**Phase variants:** early bird = "Black Friday came early" + the early offer, product leads (Lucky Early BF #10 and #14,
Dartee Early BF C). Weekend = the full offer. Monday = same ad, sticker swapped to "LAST DAY" (see template 8).
**References:**
- Ours: Lucky BFCM Static #1 (ad 120240136417930221) black and gold "BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF SITEWIDE / NO CODE NEEDED", $2,649, 32 orders, $83 CPA, 1.74 ROAS. Lucky Static #8 (offer over a gold wedge with a green gift bow, $92) and #20 ("Gold Looks Better At 50% OFF", $98). Dartee BFCM #2 Still (120233583014550741) "40% OFF SITEWIDE BLACK FRIDAY, NO CODE NEEDED" over one belt on white, $55 CPA, 1.84. Lucky Early BF #10 (120239633402980221) "Black Friday Came Early. So did the birdies." night shot, $55, 2.25. Dartee Early BF C (120232805385170741) "SMART GOLFERS SHOP EARLY / 25% OFF + FREE HAT", row of belts, $48, 1.87.
- Outside: Manscaped 1083977959386937 (gold script "Black Friday Sale / 25% OFF Entire Order + Free Shipping", 17 days, run again 2025); Bad Birdie 1589709372202591 (giant lowercase "black friday" on black, "UP TO 50% OFF"); Pins & Aces 1343426577235033 ("UP TO 50% OFF" sideways over a lit bag, 34 days); Stix Golf 541505638865903 ("Black Friday | 30% off Sitewide", 40 days).
**Route:** Lucky = Use as the ad on an Air photo. Others = AI make, or Canva master when the offer has a price.
**Brands:** all seven. The workhorse of every weekend batch: about a third of each 25.

### 2. Winner + offer badge
**Phase:** the weekend, early access, run-back. Also the cheapest ad in any batch.
**Layout:** the brand's best evergreen ad, untouched, with the offer added as a corner badge or a solid bar along the
bottom (about 15% of the height). The hook stays the hero.
**Slots:** `ORIGINAL` the evergreen winner (from Locus, judged ad set first) · `BADGE` the offer in 3 to 6 words
("BLACK FRIDAY / 40% OFF") · `END` optional.
**References:**
- Ours: Lucky Mallet Putter A (120238543970790221) "FORGIVENESS / Never Looked This Good", anchor 89%, $99 CPA through BFCM with no offer. Dartee 238 A creator video, $50 CPA, 74 orders, no offer.
- Outside: Ridge 4109362552650583 "LEATHER IS DEAD" + a black "BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 47% OFF" box, 50 days, the longest-running sale image found. True Classic 1454013189032903 ran its normal product creative with "Early Black Friday" in the copy, 23 days. Alex Cooper's rule: keep the evergreen winners on, add a holiday layer.
**Route:** Use as the ad on the winning image (Studio adds only the badge). For a video winner, Ravo adds the bar.
**Brands:** all. Each brand chat lists its top 3 evergreen anchors and badges them; the originals keep running unbadged.

### 3. Early access sign-up
**Phase:** list build, early access. Before the offer is locked, the access IS the offer.
**Layout:** a tease line on top, the reason to join today in the middle, a solid sign-up bar at the bottom. One real
product or lifestyle photo. Meta Instant Form or a Klaviyo landing page, button "Sign Up".
**Slots:**
- `TEASE` "OUR BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR IS COMING" or "48 HOURS BEFORE EVERYONE ELSE"
- `REASON` why sign up now: first pick, extra 5%, free studs over $100, the black putter before it sells out
- `DATE` when access opens ("THURSDAY NOV 26")
- `CTA BAR` "GET EARLY ACCESS"
- Optional teased number ("UP TO 50% OFF") only once the offer is locked
**References:**
- Ours: no 2025 sign-up ads; early access as a sale (Lucky Early BF, Dartee Early BF) was the cheapest set (rule 2 above).
- Outside: Craftd 835539545878951 (hand holding a sticky note "BLACK FRIDAY up to 71% OFF" over rings, white box "SIGN UP TODAY TO GET AN EXTRA 5% OFF", black "SIGN UP NOW FOR EARLY ACCESS" bar, 17 days). Crew Clothing 828572062963886 ("SIGN UP NOW for early access", 19 days). Arms Of Eve 795516423314852 ("SIGN UP AND SAVE / VIP access", 12 days). Pit Viper 1192371116161414 (early access + double points as the reason, 10 days). Jones Road 2023 notify page: 20,000 sign-ups, 375,000 kits in four days at full price.
**Route:** Lucky = Use as the ad. Others = AI make.
**Brands:** Dartee list build (3) and early access (3), Lucky early access (3), Ice & Gold gift list + early access (15) and early access (3), Grunk lead list (15), Bonk / Yak / Party Patch while their offers wait.

### 4. Black Friday only drop (the reveal)
**Phase:** list build (reveal), the weekend or Cyber Monday (it goes on sale), December drops.
**Layout:** the new product alone, dark set, dramatic light, near-full frame. One or two words naming it, one line saying
when and where it can be bought. No discount anywhere.
**Slots:** `NAME` ("ECLIPSE BLACK", "THE LIZARD") · `WHEN` ("ONLY ON BLACK FRIDAY", "DROPS CYBER MONDAY") · `ASK`
("JOIN THE LIST", "FIRST 48 HOURS: LIST ONLY") · optional `COUNT` ("200 MADE").
**References:**
- Ours: hero + one line is step 1's top format (Grunk "White Neon Rose" $50 vs $64 average, Lucky Black Wedges -9 $76 vs $90).
- Outside: Vice 1088531652970170, an exclusive Black Friday release of all-black irons. Jones Road Black-Friday-only kits (2023 and 2024). Malbon "ARCHIVE SALE" 1140012971637382: two words on the image, everything else in the copy.
**Route:** Lucky = Use as the ad on real black putter photos (samples; stock lands mid-November). Others = AI make on the product photo.
**Brands:** Lucky Eclipse Black reveal (10), Dartee Cyber Monday + Lizard drop (5), Grunk December drops (10), Lucky Havoc launch (5), Yak 2027 line tease (5).

### 5. The box (value stack / bundle)
**Phase:** early Black Friday, the weekend. Best for brands that will not cut the hero's price.
**Layout:** everything in the deal laid flat or in a fake cart, each item with its value, the total struck through, then
what you pay (or "FREE") in the biggest type. Can be a phone checkout screen.
**Slots:** `ITEMS` 3 to 5 real products with `VALUE` each · `TOTAL` struck through · `YOU PAY` · `NAME` of the bundle
("EVERYTHING A GOLFER NEEDS") · `END` optional.
**References:**
- Ours: step 1 price ladder / bundle wins: Bonk 245 B $28 CPA ($39 average), Grunk 326-6 free polo with a caddy $24, Party Patch 400-6 $29. Not run at BFCM 2025.
- Outside: Sunday Golf 1563130698454186 (fake phone checkout "FILL THE CART", bag + hat + towel + pouch, "$235" struck to "$158", red "UP TO 33% OFF!" bar, about 55 days) and 1472067703480309 (BF bundles, "What's included?" list, 23 days, renamed Christmas Bundles Dec 4 to 31). Active Truth 1753125578734037 (offer + bullet list of gifts and free shipping, 32 days). Mei's "Value stack" format.
**Route:** Canva master (the numbers are exact). Background and product shot: Use as the ad (Lucky) or AI make.
**Brands:** Bonk ($99 bundle, $130.50 value), Lucky (club + towel + divot tool with their values), Grunk (BOGO shown as two polos, one price), Yak (2 hoodies + free snapback), Party Patch once an offer exists.

### 6. Spend more, save more (the ladder)
**Phase:** the weekend.
**Layout:** three steps side by side or stacked, each a product count or spend level with its saving; the top step
visually biggest. Products in a row under the steps.
**Slots:** `STEP 1/2/3` ("2 CLUBS $50 OFF", "3+ CLUBS $100 OFF" or "$100 SAVE $25 / $200 SAVE $60 / $300 SAVE $100") ·
`EXTRA` what every order gets ("SHIPS FREE", "FREE GIFT BOX") · `EVENT`.
**References:**
- Ours: step 1 price ladder (Party Patch 400-6, PP_411, 400-4, all $29 to $31 vs $32 goal). Missed on Lucky once (366 A bundle grid $70), so Lucky's ladder stays two steps and short.
- Outside: BYLT 1083284243581680 (tiered gift card row: extra $20 / $30 / $50 at $100 / $150 / $250). Knix 618192957577182 "Buy More = Save More" (37 days). Bad Birdie 860529123580004 "Spend $150, Get $25 back".
**Route:** Canva master.
**Brands:** Lucky weekend ($50 off 2, $100 off 3+), Ice & Gold weekend (3 tiers + gift box), Bonk (buy 2 get 1 free), Party Patch if a tier offer comes.

### 7. Native phone (text, Notes, story reply, sticky note)
**Phase:** early access, the weekend, gifting.
**Layout:** looks like a screenshot, not an ad: an iMessage thread, a Notes list, an Instagram story reply, or a
handwritten sticky note on the product. Low-fi on purpose. The product appears in a photo inside the thread or behind the note.
**Slots:** `THREAD` 2 to 4 short messages between friends, partners or a golf group ("did you see Dartee's doing a free
Raincheck with every belt?") · `PHOTO` the product · `OFFER` said in a message, not designed · Notes variant: `TITLE`
("Black Friday list") + 3 products and what comes free.
**References:**
- Ours: never tested (step 1: notes app and native screenshots, 3 to 4 ads, none won, too few to judge). This is the BFCM test.
- Outside: True Classic 1338900594451417 (iMessage screenshot on black, reaction bar showing, "they've got a 70% off sale for Black Friday!!!", 20 days). TravisMathew 1370425287773504 (story reply "omg where did he get that polo from??" / "*i* gifted it to him", 36 days). Craftd sticky note (template 3). Sunday Golf 1151573966552026 (giant lime sticky note "BLACK FRIDAY IS HERE!", about 55 days). Mei's notes app, fake text thread, sticky note, reply to comment, flip phone formats.
**Route:** AI make with Studio's "native" style (Lucky: the product photo inside the thread is a real photo; the model only builds the phone frame).
**Brands:** all. Each weekend batch carries 2 or 3 of these as the test; promote the format in Locus if it wins.

### 8. Last call (Cyber Monday and the final hours)
**Phase:** Cyber Monday, the last day of any phase.
**Layout:** the weekend banner (template 1) with a loud "LAST DAY" / "ENDS TONIGHT" / "ONE DAY" sticker, or a countdown
in units ("312 OF 400 LEFT"). Optionally a day pun or a price ceiling.
**Slots:** `URGENCY` ("LAST DAY", "ENDS MIDNIGHT", "FINAL HOURS") · `OFFER` · `COUNT` optional (units left, real only) ·
`SECOND CHANCE` copy line ("Missed Friday? Today is the last day.").
**References:**
- Ours: Dartee BFCM #3 UGC ($53 CPA, 89 orders, ran Nov 29 to Dec 2) and the BFCM statics carried Monday with no new creative; "WE WILL SELL OUT" (Dartee BFCM #13 Still, $48, 2.57) is the scarcity line that worked.
- Outside: True Classic 1376222460961674 ("ONE DAY / Up To 70% Off", headline "Final Day to Save", Dec 1 to 4) and 1219661842427320 ("If you missed the Black Friday sale, now is your chance"). Chubbies 1626409934970265 ("THIGHBER MONDAY / EVERYTHING IS $40 OR LESS", a pun and a price ceiling). Pit Viper, Bad Birdie, Pins & Aces made no Monday creative: their Friday ads just ran on.
**Route:** Studio "Change the words" on the approved weekend ads (same picture, new sticker), so it costs almost nothing. Build both versions in the weekend batch (Season: "two sticker versions").
**Brands:** all. Grunk polo flash sale (5) and Grunk 24-hour deal (5) are this template with their own offer.

### 9. The gift for them
**Phase:** gifting, from the weekend into December.
**Layout:** the product as a gift: wrapped, in a hand, under a tree, half out of the box. The headline is spoken by the
BUYER about the golfer, not to the golfer.
**Slots:** `WHO` the person ("your husband", "your playing partner", "the golfer who has everything", "the coworker who
doesn't golf") · `LINE` why it lands ("The gift he'll actually wear") · `PRICE POINT` optional ("GIFTS UNDER $50") ·
`DEADLINE STAMP` optional (template 10).
**References:**
- Ours: Dartee 2023 "Shopping for that golfer in your life?" ran 61 days (Atria 3193454104290449); step 1 moment wrapper is the cheapest bucket ($36 a sale), Bonk "Retire his old golf cap" $21.
- Outside: True Classic 1348483472989224 (caption "If you don't have a gift yet, get your husband THIS" over hands unwrapping a box under a tree, 52 days). TravisMathew 2839247112948298 ("Gifts Under $50", hats on presents, 35 days). Huckberry 1088781946465040 (split "GIFT THIS / GET THIS" + "Guaranteed U.S. Christmas Delivery" stamp). Bad Birdie 1279756270014486 ("the perfect gift for your playing partner", 48 days).
**Route:** Lucky = Dress a real photo (the Dress step adds wrapping and the tree around the real product), then Use as the ad. Others = AI make.
**Brands:** all; strongest for Dartee, Bonk, Ice & Gold, Grunk. Lucky apparel + Christmas (10), I&G holiday sets (20), Dartee gifting (10), Bonk and Party Patch December.

**Slots:** `DATE` ("ORDER BY DEC 15", "LAST CHANCE FOR CHRISTMAS DELIVERY") · `PROMISE` ("ARRIVES BY CHRISTMAS") · `PRODUCT` · `BADGE` optional.
**References:**
- Ours: none run in 2025.

### 11. Last-minute gift card
**Phase:** gift cards (after the shipping cutoff, to Dec 24).
**Layout:** the brand's gift card (a clean card mock) over the product's world, or a mock email / phone notification
"Your gift card has arrived". One line about speed.
**Slots:** `HOOK` ("MISSED THE CUTOFF?", "LAST-MINUTE SHOPPING MADE EASY") · `SPEED` ("ARRIVES IN MINUTES BY EMAIL") ·
`CARD` with the brand logo · `BONUS` optional ("BUY $100, GET $20").
**References:**
- Ours: none run. No golf brand in the research ran a gift card ad.
- Outside: SITKA 9139982029424890 (black card over a misty forest, "Skip the Shipping with Gift Cards", Dec 18 to 25). Archies 3344655565670676 ("Missed the delivery cut off?" + a mock Gmail notification). BYLT 1083284243581680 (bonus card tiers). All ran 2 to 11 days: a last-week tool, keep it to 3 to 5 ads.
**Route:** AI make for the scene; the card itself is the brand's real card art (Canva master).
**Brands:** Lucky (5), Ice & Gold (5), Dartee matched gift cards (3).

### 12. Run-back (missed it / the new year)
**Phase:** Boxing Day + run-back to Dec 31, New Year.
**Layout:** two versions.
(a) **Missed it**: "Missed Black Friday?" over the weekend's best ad, offer brought back for non-buyers.
(b) **The resolution card**: a joke note taped over a festive or golf scene ("Sorry if your New Year's resolution is to
play less golf"), products below.
**Slots:** (a) `HOOK` "MISSED IT?" · `OFFER` · `END` "THROUGH DEC 31". (b) `JOKE` one or two lines · `PRODUCTS` 1 to 3.
**References:**
- Outside: Sunday Golf 1171438638450270 (taped card "SORRY IF YOUR NEW YEAR'S RESOLUTION IS TO PLAY LESS GOLF", ran 270 days) and 721941860964996 ("The 'I'm the only coworker that doesn't golf' Starter Kit", 197 days). ## Rules for every template

- The product leads; the free gift is the second line, never the picture (Dartee "FREE HAT?" lost).
- "No code needed" or "ships free" on every offer banner where it is true.
- One offer per ad. If the image says 50% the copy says 50% (Party Patch BFCM #15 said 50% on the image and 40% in the copy).
- No offer ad until the offer is locked (game plan rule 1): templates 2 (without the badge), 3, 4, 9 can run before.
- Hooks that ride on any template, from Cole's formats: Apology note ("finance is not happy about the free polos"),
  "Do not buy this without the free hat", Strikethrough (10%, 20%, 30% struck out, then the real deal), mocking Black
  Friday itself (Bad Birdie's "no fake countdown timers"). These are headline lines, not layouts.
- Formats from the 17 not given a template, on purpose: Google search, meme, tweet, Reddit post, email screenshot.
  None showed up in the winners anywhere; a brand chat may test one inside template 7.
