  Look facts: Carver 02 Gold = gold head, GOLD shaft, WHITE grip. Stryker = BLACK shaft with a gold band, BLACK grip.
  Eclipse = gold head, GOLD shaft, thick WHITE grip. Logo = L, clover, CKY. Air lifestyle shots 15 and 39 show a dark
  shaft (older wedge): do not use them as the Carver 02.

  hand a club is for, no invented numbers.

Lessons: (1) at Black Friday the OFFER is the headline, said once, big, plain; feature-led ads with the offer as a badge
lose. (2) Single hero product on a dark or clean ground wins all year. (3) Creators to camera hold ~2x. (4) The 2025
discount bought orders at a loss (week down 35% on a deeper cut): this year's offer is gifts + money off only on 2 and 3
clubs, so every ad must make the ladder obvious.

**Competitors (no names in copy).** From the website chat's verified research (v9/giveaway/CREATIVE-BRIEF.md) and the
plan page: the closest DTC brand gave an extra (a free lesson) instead of a discount and charges shipping on every order;
another gave a 10% reward on clubs; set and iron brands ran 10 to 20% off; the big names ran $100 off putters or drivers.
Lucky's $50 / $100 off on 2 / 3 clubs is deeper than all of them but only for bigger orders, and the free towel + divot
tool is the "extra" move. **Still to do:** an ad-by-ad pass of last year's golf Black Friday creative in Atria (the
connector was rate-limiting on Oct 13; re-run, then add a reference ad id to each line below).

Offer: full price; free towel with any club; two or more clubs add a divot tool. Glove $9.99 in the cart.
Note: the giveaway's code FOURSOME (free hat with any club) also runs to Nov 16. Two gifts at once; decide whether
Early Bird ads mention the hat code (recommend no: one gift per ad).
1. **Gift in the box.** Angle: the club arrives with a towel. Why: single hero on a dark ground is the account's best
   static (Black Wedges set); gifts beat discounts on margin. Concept: real Carver cutout + real towel photo (needs the
   towel shot, plan shot 5) on black, "Every club ships with a towel. Through Nov 16." Testing: concept. Template: hero +
   one line. Reference: Black Wedges 9.
2. **Two clubs, two gifts.** Angle: buy two, the divot tool comes too. Why: two-club orders are the margin step (2025
   early 30% off did nothing; a gift ladder is new). Concept: two Carvers + towel + divot tool laid out like a receipt,
   "1 club: towel. 2 clubs: towel + divot tool." Testing: concept. Template: price ladder (gifts, no prices off).
3. **Early Bird, creator.** Angle: a creator unboxes a club and finds the towel. Why: creators hold ~2x (Jett, Kerr, BF
   UGC). Concept: Michael Kerr or Jett films the box open; supers "Free towel, any club, through Nov 16". Testing:
   format (first creator Early Bird). Template: creator to camera. Needs: creator booked by Oct 20.
