How it works

What happens after you tag it

About a minute of work you'd normally spend rewatching, transcribing and formatting. Here's what it does, in order.

🎬
Watches the video once

Writes down the hook, every scene, the words on screen, what's said, and any visual trick that grabs attention (a prop, a weird action, a text pop).

📝
Reads your note and the whole thread

Your note always wins over its own guess. If you said "just the hook", it only takes the hook.

🧠
Pulls up the brand brain

Who buys this product, real customer words from reviews, the competitors, and what we've already tested and how it did.

🔍
Figures out why it works

The hook, the desire, how aware the viewer is, the proof. Then it decides the take: use as is, borrow the style or just the hook, and flags what NOT to copy.

✍️
Writes the draft for the best home

Picks creator link, Asana or Studio, and writes it short. If it's missing something important, it asks you 1 to 3 questions first.

What you get back

One short card

  • The idea in a title and one line
  • Who it's for and the opener
  • Buttons to send it where you want
  • Details button for the full teardown

The buttons on the card

Creator link (suggested) sends it to its best home (green = suggested)
Make Asana brief draft writes a version for another home
Section which section of the creator link
Redo writes it again from scratch
Details full breakdown in the thread
Discard drops it
Want changes? Just reply in the thread ("make the opener funnier, use the travel pack") and tag it again. It revises without rewatching the video.

Cheat sheet

Keep this one open

What to write in your note

"as is" copy the whole idea for our brand
"the style" keep the look and format, new message
"just the hook" only the first 3 seconds
"for ambassadors" creator link / TRYBE
"Asana, for the designer" static brief
"video, for the editor" video brief, our footage
"Studio" AI makes the statics
"quick" faster, cheaper draft

What you can drop in

  • A video uploaded straight to Slack (best)
  • An Atria or Meta Ad Library link
  • A YouTube link
  • A screenshot of a static
  • No link at all: just describe the idea
  • A whole thread of back and forth: tag it at the end and it reads everything

TikTok and IG links: save the video and upload it to Slack.

Pick the right home

Creator link

Real people on camera, low production: TRYBE ambassadors, affiliates, creators.

Asana brief

Our team builds it: designer statics, editor videos with B-roll, high-production shoots, or creators we brief directly.

Studio

AI makes statics only in Locus. Your inspo image comes along. Videos go to the creator link or Asana.

How many ads it writes

Creator link

1 angle: 2 openers, 3 scenes.

Asana brief

3 to 5 ads, one per line.

Studio

3 to 6 ads, one per line (up to 12).

Want a set number? Just say it: "6 headlines", "2 videos".

Always read it before you send it. It's a strong first draft, not the final word. Your strategy and taste still decide.
Cost: about 15 to 20 cents an idea. Normal chat in the channel costs nothing.
