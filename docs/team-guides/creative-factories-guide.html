The four factories

Pick the factory that fits the idea

🤖

AI Studio

Use it when you want lots of statics fast: many headlines, many angles.

Who: StrategistWhere: Locus, StudioCost: ~35¢ an ad
🤳

Ambassadors

Use it when a real person needs to say it: UGC, reviews, real-life use.

Who: Strategist sets anglesWhere: creator link (TRYBE)Lucky: Lucky creator app
🧩

Templates

Use it when a layout already won. Refill it with a new angle or product.

Who: StrategistWhere: Locus, StudioSource: last month's winners
🎨

Designer and production

Use it when it has to be perfect or it's a video: hero work, launches, B-roll edits.

Who: Designer, video editorWhere: Asana briefSpeed: 2 to 3 days
Shortcut: drop inspo in Slack and tag the Ideas bot. It writes the brief for whichever factory fits (see the Ideas bot guide).
