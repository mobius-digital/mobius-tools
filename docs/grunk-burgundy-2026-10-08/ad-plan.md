# Grunk Dolfer: Burgundy Drinko launch ads (25 statics)

Launch: Oct 13. Brand: Grunk Dolfer. Paste this whole doc into Locus Studio > Bulk from a plan; it splits into the 6 batches below.

The collection (live on the store since Oct 1):
- Mr. Burgundy Driver Cover, $49.95 (anchorman face, 3D mustache). THE HERO. He is our Ron.
- Burgundy Drinko Polo, $69.95 (white base, burgundy tequila/taco/cactus/sugar skull print)
- Burgundy Donde Esta La Cart Girl Hat, $39.95 (cream embroidery, rope)
- Burgundy Drinko Hoodie, $89.95 (Drinko print inside the hood, side pockets)
- Burgundy Drinko Mini Den Caddy, $249.99 (18 in. insulated cooler caddy)
- Sex Panther Fairway Wood Cover (in Air, not on the store yet)
- Burgundy I'm Drunk Hybrid Cover (in Air, not on the store yet)

Rules for every ad:
- 4:5, text inside the 1:1 safe zone.
- ONE hero product per ad unless the line says lineup. Real proportions: a driver cover is smaller than a polo; a Den Caddy is 18 in. tall.
- The Mr. Burgundy driver cover plays Ron. No Will Ferrell face, no movie stills, no Channel 4 or Paramount logos. Riff on the lines, never paste a scene.
- Each batch is its own visual world so Meta reads them as different ads (Andromeda). No two ads share a layout.

## Batch 1: Channel 4 Grunk News (the newsroom world)
Angle: the launch IS the news. Concept: classic 70s local TV news, wood-panel desk, CRT glow, chyrons.
Testing: news formats as the launch hook.
1. Breaking news broadcast. Driver cover sits at the anchor desk with a mic. Chyron: "BREAKING: Local driver cover is kind of a big deal." Small: "Burgundy Drinko Collection. Live Oct 13."
2. Weather report. Hoodie on a mannequin in front of a green-screen course map, cartoon suns and beer icons. Headline: "Today's forecast: 100% chance of day drinking." Small: "Hoodie weather. Mostly burgundy."
3. Teleprompter gag. Over-the-shoulder shot of a teleprompter. Prompter text: "Stay classy, first tee." Small: "He'll read anything you put in front of him. Including your scorecard."
4. Newspaper front page. Vintage paper, polo photo above the fold. Headline: "LOCAL MAN DRESSED BETTER THAN HIS ROUND." Sub: "Witnesses describe pattern as 'tequila, tacos and a triple bogey.'"
5. 70s magazine cover. "SAN DIEGO GOLF MONTHLY" style masthead (call it GRUNK MONTHLY), driver cover as the cover star, cover lines: "60% of the time, it fairways every time" / "The mustache: an investigation" / "Where is the cart girl?"
