# Bonk Golf BFCM 2026: ad concepts per batch (Oct 13)

Owner: the Bonk BFCM ads chat. One numbered line = one ad (Mobius framework: Angle, Why, Concept, What we're testing).
Offers and dates: Locus Season `brand_bonk_golf`; Asana "BFCM 2026" section "Bonk Golf (locked, Floyd to confirm)";
build calendar: docs/bfcm-ads-gameplan-2026-10-13.md. Template names are the 8 formats from the creative-templates
analysis (hero + one line, feature callouts, scene + bullets, price ladder, moment wrapper, review over product,
demo/unbox, creator to camera); docs/bfcm-ad-templates.md is not written yet.

**Every line is tagged:** `BUILD NOW` (no offer in it), `NEEDS OFFER` (Floyd's confirmed offer goes in the words),
`NEEDS DATES` (shipping cutoffs or drop dates). The offer lines get built the day Cole says Floyd answered.

## Floyd's rules (from the brand brain; he has threatened to fire a creative team over the first one)
- **No AI-generated imagery, ever.** Only real bonkgolf.com studio/product photos and Bonk's own lifestyle shoots
  (background removal is fine). This is stricter than the gameplan's "other brands may use AI product drawing": for
  Bonk, Studio's image model may not draw the scene either. So Bonk statics are built in HTML around real Shopify
  pixels (like the Lucky fyf ads) and uploaded to Studio as "the photo IS the ad" lines. Zero image-model spend.
- Simple studio product shots win ("It's always the simple ones that seem to work best"). The numbers agree (below).
- Only current, in-stock, correctly shown products (tee slot and magnet placement, fitted vs adjustable, Southpaw hats in
  Southpaw ads only). Offers must be literal ("Site Wide" was pulled after complaints).
- Never claim "Sweat Stain Resistant" on PF 2.0. Check "patented" vs "patent-pending" with Floyd before using either.
- No competitor names or logos. 4:5 canvas, all words inside the 1:1 safe zone. No em dashes.

Lessons: (1) last year the evergreen product heroes beat the offer ads all through BFCM; the B2G1 statics were the
weakest thing running. (2) Early BF ($36k) out-earned the weekend ($16.7k) by 2x. (3) The offer ad that worked said
it in five words over real hats. So: keep the proven heroes running through the weekend with an offer sticker on them
(the Asana task's "+2 sticker versions"), and make the bundle ads about the gift, not the discount.
