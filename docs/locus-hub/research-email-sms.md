### 1d. Benchmarks (Analytics > Benchmarks)

- Peer groups are "a set of roughly one hundred companies that are similar to your own in size and scope" matched on industry, average item value, revenue, growth rate, campaign frequency and email revenue percentage; set via Organization > Contact Information. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Requirements: at least 25 emails sent in the last 6 months for email benchmarks, at least 25 SMS for SMS benchmarks, an industry set; refreshed monthly on the 10th. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Five pages: Overview (top and bottom performing metrics by percentile), Business performance (average cart size, average order count, average order value, percentage of orders returned; your value, Median (Peers), 25th / 50th / 75th percentiles), Campaigns, Flows, Sign-up forms. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Campaigns page email metrics: open rate, click rate, click through rate, conversion rate, email revenue per recipient, bounce rate, spam report rate, unsubscribe rate. SMS: click rate, conversion rate, revenue per recipient, unsubscribe rate. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Flows page: same metric sets, grouped as All Flows, Welcome series, Abandoned cart, Browse abandonment, Thank you flows, or an individual flow; peer comparison is matched by flow type. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Sign-up forms page: submit rate (submissions divided by views), popup forms only. Status tiers: Poor below the 25th percentile, Fair 25th to 50th, Good 50th to 75th, Excellent above the 75th. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Chart colors: blue is you, green is the peer median, yellow is the industry median, dashed blue is Klaviyo best practice guidance. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Benchmark rates are aggregated, not averaged: clicks from all campaigns divided by deliveries from all campaigns. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050110072
- Peer groups are "a set of 100 companies that are similar to your own in industry, size, and scope (e.g., average item value, total revenue, year over year growth rate) and campaign characteristics (e.g., percent of days with a campaign sent and revenue percentage)". https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360050180151

### 1e. Deliverability hub (Analytics > Deliverability)

- Email hub tabs: Score, Reports, Bounce Details. The score (0 to 100) is built from the last 30 days of open rate, click rate, bounce rate, unsubscribe rate and spam complaint rate; tiers Poor 0 to 49, Fair 50 to 74, Good 75 to 89, Excellent 90 to 100; an action center lists fixes in priority order. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/18378819907995
- Klaviyo's published deliverability thresholds: bounce rate Healthy under 1.0%, Room for improvement 1 to 2%, Needs attention over 2%; unsubscribe rate Healthy under 0.3%, 0.3 to 1%, over 1%; spam complaint rate Healthy under 0.01%, 0.01 to 0.05%, over 0.05%; open rate target over 33% for campaigns (flows average 55.21%); click rate target over 1% for campaigns (flows average 5.37%). https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000201131
- Deliverability metric formulas: unique open rate is opens divided by delivered; unique click rate is clicks divided by delivered; bounce rate is bounces divided by sent; unsubscribe rate is unsubscribes divided by delivered; spam complaint rate is complaints divided by delivered. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000201131
- Monitoring cadence Klaviyo recommends: daily campaign segment reports, benchmarks and provider trends; weekly Google Postmaster Tools (and Outlook SNDS for dedicated IPs); monthly seed list and content tests; quarterly deliverability audit. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000201131
- SMS deliverability hub: Delivery rate, Fail rate, Click rate, Unsubscribe rate; alerts when Device Disconnected, Device Unreachable, Message Blocked or Device Incapable failures exceed 5% of sends, Carrier Violation or Unknown errors exceed 10%, click rate drops below 6.0%, unsubscribe rate rises above 1.3%, or a toll free number is unverified; filters for 7, 30, 90 days or custom, campaigns vs flows, SMS vs MMS; cards for Alerts, Key metrics, Fail details, Recent campaigns (Healthy vs Needs attention), Flows. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/21206358003355

### 1g. Predictive analytics and customer lifetime value

- Predictive properties: Historic CLV (all previous orders, net of refunds and returns), Predicted CLV (spend in the next year), Total CLV (historic plus predicted), Churn Risk Prediction, Average Time Between Orders, Predicted Gender, Expected Date of Next Order. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020919731
- Requirements: "At least 500 customers have placed an order", an ecommerce integration or API placed orders, at least 180 days of order history with orders in the last 30 days, and "At least some customers who have placed 3 or more orders". The CLV model "retrains the model at least once a week". Expected date of next order needs at least two orders from that customer. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020919731
- Churn probability drops after each order and rises as time since the last order grows; shown as low (green), medium (yellow), high (red). https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020919731
- Glossary: CLV is "the total amount, both past and predicted, that a customer will purchase from your brand over time". https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832

### 1i. Attribution: how Klaviyo credits revenue, and why it differs from Shopify and Triple Whale

- Model: last touch inside a cooperative multi channel framework; "we give each Klaviyo owned channel their own distinct configurable window and non-Klaviyo channels a single configurable window"; Klaviyo "will only attribute a customer event (e.g., placed order) to messages within one of these open windows"; the three interactions that count are opens, clicks and deliveries; the window starts when the profile receives the message; attribution recalculates within 3 hours and can update for up to 5 days for late events. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260804504250
- Default windows (Settings > Attribution): email clicks 5 days, email opens 5 days, SMS clicks 5 days, SMS opens 1 day, SMS deliveries 12 hours, push opens 24 hours, WhatsApp clicks 5 days, WhatsApp opens 12 hours, Active on Site 1 day (Advanced KDP and Marketing Analytics only). Windows go up to 90 days or 720 hours; you choose which interactions count per channel; bot clicks are excluded by default for new accounts; an option "Removes Apple Privacy Protection (MPP) opens from your attribution"; "any changes to attribution will apply to both historical and future conversions" and take up to 36 hours; a Model Comparison tool previews a change. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/11118357030555
- Klaviyo groups attributed revenue by send date: "attributed revenue is grouped by the send date of the campaign or flow message, and not when the order took place", so an August 1 send that converts on August 4 is counted on August 1 (Klaviyo staff). https://community.klaviyo.com/developer-group-64/how-to-retrieve-attributed-conversions-for-campaigns-and-flows-via-the-aggregation-api-18205
- Triple Whale on the difference: Klaviyo "Uses a 5-day attribution window" and "Attributes conversions based on email opens and clicks. This includes cases where an email was opened but never clicked"; Triple Whale "Uses a lifetime attribution window, attributing revenue only when a user clicks an email" and "Relies on UTMs or custom parameters to attribute an order to Klaviyo. If these UTMs are missing or not properly appended, Triple Whale may attribute the sale to '(not set)'"; Apple MPP "False opens" inflate Klaviyo's open based numbers; switching Klaviyo to "Clicked Email Only" aligns closest to Triple Whale. https://triplewhale.readme.io/docs/why-is-my-klaviyo-or-emailsms-revenue-in-triple-whale-different-from-the-klaviyo-dashboard
- Shopify's default is last non direct click on a 30 day window, versus Klaviyo's last touch with 5 day email windows (reconciliation guide). https://weltpixel.com/blogs/news/how-to-reconcile-klaviyo-attributed-revenue-with-ga4-and-shopify
- Polar's position: "Klaviyo over-credits itself" through last click; Polar "re-attributes with its own first-party Pixel and de-duplicates against paid and organic" so revenue is "counted once". https://www.polaranalytics.com/integrations/klaviyo

### 1j. Glossary definitions (the denominators)

- Recipients: "the count of messages that were sent to recipients over all time". Delivered: emails delivered, excluding bounces; delivery rate is deliveries divided by recipients. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Bounce rate: "the number of bounced addresses divided by the total number of recipients". https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Open rate: "the number of individuals opening your email divided by the number of recipients"; Opened (%) on dashboards is unique opens divided by deliveries. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Click rate: "the percentage of people who clicked a link in your message out of the people received it"; Click-through rate (CTR): clicked out of those who opened. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Placed order rate: "orders placed within the channel's attribution window divided by the number of unique recipients". https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Revenue per recipient: "your total revenue attributed to an email or SMS for a given timeframe divided by the total number of people who have received a message". https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Average order value: conversion value divided by the number of conversions. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Revenue from Klaviyo (KAV): "the sum of all Klaviyo attribute conversions for a select timeframe". Total revenue: "the sum of all revenue for a select timeframe". https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Unsubscribe rate: unsubscribes divided by recipients, "measured on a per campaign basis"; Spam complaint rate: complaints divided by recipients. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832
- Subscribed to List and Unsubscribed from List are the list growth metrics (events). https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832

## 2. Klaviyo REST API coverage

### 2a. Reporting API

- The Reporting API has seven report endpoints: Campaign Values, Flow Values, Flow Series, Form Values, Form Series, Segment Values, Segment Series. There is no campaign series report (the URL /reference/query_campaign_series returns 404). https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/reporting_api_overview
- conversion_metric_id is "The ID of the conversion metric (e.g., the metric ID for Placed Order) used for calculating conversion-based statistics"; non conversion statistics are returned regardless. "performance in the Klaviyo UI is calculated based on send date (the date that a campaign or flow message sends)" and the API groups by send date the same way. Max timeframe 1 year; a custom end "rounds up to include the current hour", so set end to the last second of the day. Series intervals are daily, hourly, weekly or monthly and default to weekly. SMS credit statistics (message_segment_count_sum, text_message_credit_usage_amount, text_message_roi, text_message_spend) "only apply to campaigns and flows containing text messages" and are null before October 1 2025 for contracted plans. failed_rate is "Failed SMS / (Received SMS + Failed SMS)". https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/reporting_api_overview
- Klaviyo staff: pass "your Placed Order metric id (or whatever conversion metric you want to use"; for Shopify it is Placed Order with the Shopify icon. https://community.klaviyo.com/developer-group-64/campaign-values-reports-endpoint-14828
- Klaviyo staff: "If you want to replicate the frontend reporting, take a look at the reporting API endpoint" rather than metric aggregates. https://community.klaviyo.com/developer-group-64/how-to-retrieve-attributed-conversions-for-campaigns-and-flows-via-the-aggregation-api-18205

**POST /api/campaign-values-reports**
- Statistics (30): average_order_value, bounce_rate, bounced, bounced_or_failed, bounced_or_failed_rate, click_rate, click_to_open_rate, clicks, clicks_unique, conversion_rate, conversion_uniques, conversion_value, conversions, delivered, delivery_rate, failed, failed_rate, message_segment_count_sum, open_rate, opens, opens_unique, recipients, revenue_per_recipient, spam_complaint_rate, spam_complaints, text_message_credit_usage_amount, text_message_roi, text_message_spend, unsubscribe_rate, unsubscribe_uniques, unsubscribes. Rates come back as fractions 0.0 to 1.0. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_campaign_values
- Timeframe presets: last_12_months, last_30_days, last_365_days, last_3_months, last_7_days, last_90_days, last_month, last_week, last_year, this_month, this_week, this_year, today, yesterday; or custom start and end ISO 8601 ("Offset is ignored and the company timezone is used"). conversion_metric_id required. Filters: campaign_id and send_channel (email, sms, push-notification, whatsapp) as scalars; tag_id, tag_name with contains-any or contains-all. Default grouping is campaign_message_id, campaign_id, send_channel. Rate limit Burst 1/s, Steady 2/m, Daily 225/d. Response: results[] of groupings and statistics. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_campaign_values and https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/reporting_api_overview

**POST /api/flow-values-reports**
- Same 31 statistics as campaigns (the list above). Filters: flow_id, flow_name, send_channel, flow_message_id, flow_message_name, text_message_format, variation, variation_name (equals or contains-any), tag_id, tag_name (contains-any, contains-all). Group by: flow_id, flow_message_id, flow_message_name, flow_name, send_channel, tag_id, tag_name, text_message_format, variation, variation_name, with flow_id and flow_message_id required. Rate limit 1/s, 2/m, 225/d. Response type flow-values-report with results[] (groupings, statistics) and relationships to flows and flow-messages. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_flow_values

**POST /api/flow-series-reports**
- Same statistics. Intervals and max spans: hourly up to 7 days, daily up to 60 days, weekly and monthly up to 52 weeks. Filters as flow values, AND only, max 100 items per list filter. Rate limit 1/s, 2/m, 225/d. Response: results[] plus a date_times[] array aligned by index. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_flow_series

**POST /api/form-values-reports and /api/form-series-reports**
- Statistics (12): viewed_form, viewed_form_uniques, viewed_form_step, viewed_form_step_uniques, submitted_form_step, submitted_form_step_uniques, submits, submit_rate, qualified_form, qualified_form_uniques, closed_form, closed_form_uniques. Group by form_id (default) or form_version_id (needs form_id). Filters form_id, form_version_id with equals or any, max 100 per any. Custom timeframe max 1 year. Series intervals: hourly 7 days, daily 60 days, weekly and monthly 52 weeks. Rate limit 1/s, 2/m, 225/d. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_form_values and https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_form_series

**POST /api/segment-values-reports and /api/segment-series-reports**
- Statistics (4): members_added, members_removed, net_members_changed, total_members. Filter segment_id only (equals or any, max 100). Max 1 year; "data unavailable before June 1, 2023". Series intervals: hourly 7 days, daily 60 days, monthly 52 weeks. Rate limit 1/s, 2/m, 225/d. Scope segments:read. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_segment_values and https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_segment_series

### 2b. Metric aggregates (POST /api/metric-aggregates)

- Body: metric_id (required), measurements (count, unique, sum_value), filter (must include a datetime range using greater-or-equal(datetime,...) and less-than(datetime,...)), interval (hour, day, week, month; default day), by (dimensions), timezone (IANA, default UTC), page_size (default 500), page_cursor, sort, return_fields. Rate limit Burst 3/s, Steady 60/m. Scope metrics:read. Response: attributes.dates[] and attributes.data[] rows with dimensions and measurements. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_metric_aggregates
- "Maximum date range of 1 year." "Only 1 filter can be used per query in addition to date range." Example extra filters: equals($message,"ID"), not(equals($attributed_flow,"")). Fields with a "$" prefix "are reserved fields generated by Klaviyo". count is total occurrences, unique is distinct per identifier, sum_value sums the value (revenue). https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/using_the_query_metric_aggregates_endpoint
- Supported by dimensions: $attributed_channel, $attributed_flow, $attributed_message, $attributed_variation, $campaign_channel, $flow, $flow_channel, $message, $message_send_cohort, $variation, $variation_send_cohort, Bot Click, Bounce Type, Campaign Name, Client Canonical, Client Name, Client Type, Email Domain, Failure Source, Failure Type, From Number, From Phone Region, Inbox Provider, List, Message Name, Message Type, Method, Subject, To Number, To Phone Region, URL, form_id. "an empty dimensions response is expected when the counts for the events do not have the associated dimension requested." Timezone is case sensitive and validated against IANA; response datetimes are UTC. https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/supported_metrics_and_attributes
- Metrics you can aggregate (metric names as Klaviyo records them): Placed Order (the Shopify conversion metric), Received Email, Opened Email, Clicked Email, Bounced Email, Marked Email as Spam, Unsubscribed, Subscribed to List, Unsubscribed from List, Received SMS, Clicked SMS, Failed to Deliver SMS, Subscribed to SMS Marketing, Unsubscribed from SMS Marketing. Sources: glossary (Received SMS, Failed to Deliver SMS, Unsubscribed from SMS, Subscribed to List, Unsubscribed from List, Marked as Spam) https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037957832 ; SMS metrics article (Relayed SMS, Received SMS, Clicked SMS, Subscribed to SMS Marketing, Unsubscribed from SMS Marketing) https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035345572 ; Metrics API overview names Placed Order, Opened Email, Clicked Email, Sent SMS, Marked Email as Spam https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/metrics_api_overview
- Metric aggregates group Placed Order by the order's own date, which is why they differ from the UI; a Klaviyo staff example uses Placed Order with by $attributed_message and measurements sum_value, count and unique. https://community.klaviyo.com/developer-group-64/how-to-retrieve-attributed-conversions-for-campaigns-and-flows-via-the-aggregation-api-18205

## 4. The questions a retention or email strategist asks every week

- Weekly campaign report, top and bottom performers and why; weekly flow report, top and bottom performers; weekly list growth report; a channel report, email vs text; recompute click rate and revenue per recipient by send hour; rank weekdays by RPR and click rate; check whether multiple sends a day cannibalize; group subject lines by job (discount, urgency, curiosity, personalization); audit every live flow's triggers, splits, delays and channel; "Show me delivery, bounce, spam-complaint, and unsubscribe rates for every send over the last 90 days"; which segments perform best and which are dropping off; "members added, removed, and net change per month" for lists and key segments. https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/klaviyo-mcp-use-cases
- Klaviyo's own weekly report prompt asks for campaigns and flows, flows split by channel, the prior week as comparison, and these metrics: Report recipients, Conversions, Unique opens, Unique clicks, Delivered, Conversion value, Average order value, Revenue per recipient, using the Placed Order metric. https://www.klaviyo.com/ai-prompt-library/pull-weekly-performance-report
- Revenue share targets: "30% of your store's overall revenue should come from email marketing"; at least 15% of total revenue from flows and at least 15% from campaigns; review placed order rate for campaigns and flows against peers; the four core flow groups to assess are Welcome, Thank You, Browse and Abandoned Cart; form opt in rate target "between 1-5%"; advanced flows to check for include SMS, Price Drop, Back in Stock, Add to Cart and Predictive Date of Next Order. https://flowium.com/blog/klaviyo-audit-expert-guide/
- Klaviyo's audit guidance: set KPIs such as average open rate, track before and after, and compare "opens, clicks, conversions, and revenue per recipient with industry averages". https://academy.klaviyo.com/en-us/best-practices/best-practices-for-auditing-your-klaviyo-account
- Growth questions: how many new subscribers joined each month, which sources drove them, where the holes are that cause unsubscribes, by channel; which sign up form step people drop at (one example: 51% dropped at the SMS step); which SMS flows generate most of the revenue. https://community.klaviyo.com/strategic-advice-27/measuring-success-growth-opportunities-with-reports-15592
- Revenue per subscriber: monthly email attributed revenue divided by active subscribers; the common benchmark is about $1 per subscriber per month, with strong DTC programs at $1.50 to $3.00; under $1.50 reads as under monetized, $1.50 to $4.00 healthy, above $4.00 excellent; the top 20% of subscribers often produce 60 to 70% of email revenue, so split RPS by engagement tier and cohort (new, repeat, VIP, winback). https://topgrowthmarketing.com/how-much-is-your-email-list-worth/
- Seven KPIs one agency lives by: open rate, click through rate, revenue per subscriber ("it's useful to compare your subscribers to each other"), click to open rate, conversion rate, unsubscribe rate (healthy below 0.2% per send, above 0.5% signals a content or frequency problem), list growth rate. https://topgrowthmarketing.com/email-marketing-kpis
- Abandoned cart recovery rate: "Recovered orders attributed to the recovery program ÷ total abandon events × 100"; 10 to 15% is solid, 15 to 20% is advanced; reconcile with a holdout and a fixed window (7 days email, 48 hours SMS in the worked example); Klaviyo's 2023 abandoned cart flow figures quoted there: about 50.5% open, 6.25% click, 3.33% placed order, $3.65 RPR. https://attribuly.com/blogs/abandoned-cart-recovery-rate-klaviyo/
- Cadence vs unsubscribes: "Excessive cadence drives unsubs, while less than 2 sends per week leaves revenue on the table" (send cadence KPI card). https://docs.vortexiq.ai/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/klaviyo/send-cadence
- SMS program health (Postscript, 17,000+ Shopify stores, Jan 1 to Dec 15 2025): acquisition rate median about 1.06%, 75th percentile 2.32%, 90th 5.22%; 30 day retention median 93.1%, 75th 96.9%, 90th 99.1%; revenue per message median $0.98, 75th $2.13, 90th $4.54; cadence median 1.91 messages per subscriber per month, 75th 3.84, 90th 6.65; back in stock click 36 to 58% and conversion 7 to 13.8%. https://draft.postscript.io/blog/sms-marketing-benchmarks-what-good-performance-looks-like-in-2026
- SMS opt in growth is the "growth engine": "A larger consented list means more reachable recipients, so growth in opt-ins feeds every other metric". https://docs.vortexiq.ai/nerve-centre/kpi-cards/klaviyo/sms-opt-in-trend.md
- Deliverability questions to ask weekly come straight from Klaviyo's thresholds: bounce under 1%, unsubscribe under 0.3%, spam complaints under 0.01%, campaign open over 33%, campaign click over 1%. https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000201131
- Flow benchmarks to compare against each week: welcome flow email click 6.5% average; abandoned cart email RPR $6.77 average and $13.70 top 10%; SMS abandoned cart RPR $4.48. https://www.klaviyo.com/marketing-resources/abandoned-cart-benchmarks
- Flows should carry a large share of email revenue for a small share of sends (41% of revenue from 5.3% of sends across Klaviyo), so a flow share well under that is the first thing to question. https://www.klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing/benchmarks

## The screen a strategist would want

Built only from numbers the Klaviyo API returns. Each element names its endpoint. Conversion metric is the brand's Shopify Placed Order metric id from GET /api/metrics (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/get_metrics). Period selector and compare period follow the Locus date menu; Klaviyo reporting groups by send date (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/reporting_api_overview), so label every revenue figure "Klaviyo attributed, by send date". Triple Whale stays the store revenue truth and should sit beside these numbers, not be replaced by them (https://triplewhale.readme.io/docs/why-is-my-klaviyo-or-emailsms-revenue-in-triple-whale-different-from-the-klaviyo-dashboard).

Budget note: every reporting endpoint allows 225 calls a day (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_campaign_values). One brand screen needs about six reporting calls per load, so cache per brand per period for an hour or more.

### Row 1: six tiles (the answer in one line)

1. Klaviyo attributed revenue, with share of all Placed Order revenue. Numerator: conversion_value from POST /api/campaign-values-reports plus POST /api/flow-values-reports (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_campaign_values, https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_flow_values). Denominator: POST /api/metric-aggregates on Placed Order, measurement sum_value, for the same dates (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_metric_aggregates). Show the compare period delta.
2. Flows vs campaigns split. Two conversion_value numbers and the flow share; flag when the flow share is far below Klaviyo's 41% (https://www.klaviyo.com/products/email-marketing/benchmarks). Endpoints: flow-values-reports and campaign-values-reports.
3. Revenue per recipient, email and SMS side by side. revenue_per_recipient from campaign-values-reports and flow-values-reports filtered send_channel equals email, then sms (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_campaign_values).
4. Placed order rate. conversion_rate from the same two endpoints, campaigns and flows shown separately.
5. List net growth. net_members_changed and total_members from POST /api/segment-series-reports on the brand's "email subscribers" and "SMS subscribers" segments (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_segment_series); current size from GET /api/segments/{id} with additional-fields[segment]=profile_count (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/get_segment).
6. Deliverability health. bounce_rate, spam_complaint_rate, unsubscribe_rate from campaign-values-reports, colored against Klaviyo's thresholds: bounce 1% / 2%, unsubscribe 0.3% / 1%, spam 0.01% / 0.05% (https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000201131).

### Row 2: the chart (what moved)

- Daily attributed revenue, flows and campaigns stacked, compare period ghosted. Flows: POST /api/flow-series-reports, statistic conversion_value, interval daily (max 60 days; switch to weekly past that) (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_flow_series). Campaigns: there is no campaign series report, so use POST /api/metric-aggregates on Placed Order, sum_value, interval day, by $attributed_channel, filter not(equals($attributed_flow,"")) for the flow share and the complement for campaigns (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/using_the_query_metric_aggregates_endpoint). Footnote on the chart: the aggregates line is by order date, the series line is by send date (https://community.klaviyo.com/developer-group-64/how-to-retrieve-attributed-conversions-for-campaigns-and-flows-via-the-aggregation-api-18205).
- Toggle: sends and deliveries by day. metric-aggregates on Received Email and Received SMS, count, interval day (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/supported_metrics_and_attributes).

### Row 3: Campaigns table (this period, sorted by revenue)

- Columns, all from POST /api/campaign-values-reports grouped by campaign_id and send_channel: recipients, delivered, delivery_rate, open_rate, click_rate, click_to_open_rate, conversions, conversion_rate, conversion_value, average_order_value, revenue_per_recipient, unsubscribe_rate, spam_complaint_rate, bounce_rate; for SMS rows add failed_rate, text_message_spend, text_message_roi (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_campaign_values). Names and send dates from GET /api/campaigns (used the same way by Daasity, https://help.daasity.com/core-concepts/data-integrations/setup-guides/digital-integrations/klaviyo-summary-statistics/integration-specifications).
- A top and bottom marker on RPR and unsubscribe rate, matching the weekly "top and bottom performers, and why" question (https://www.klaviyo.com/blog/klaviyo-mcp-use-cases).
- Footer line: campaigns sent this week vs last, and unsubscribe rate against the 0.3% line, so cadence vs unsubscribes is visible in one glance (https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000201131).

### Row 4: Flows table (core flows first)

- Rows for Welcome, Abandoned cart, Browse abandonment, Post purchase, Winback, then every other live flow. Identify by flow name or tag through flow-values-reports filters flow_name or tag_name (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_flow_values). Columns: recipients, delivered, open_rate, click_rate, conversion_rate, conversions, conversion_value, revenue_per_recipient, unsubscribe_rate, with SMS rows adding failed_rate and text_message_roi.
- Expand a flow to see per message: group by flow_message_id with the same statistics, the Klaviyo per message view (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_flow_values).
- Reference column: Klaviyo public averages for the matching flow type (abandoned cart email RPR $6.77, click 6.0%; welcome click 6.5%; SMS abandoned cart RPR $4.48) as static text next to the brand number (https://www.klaviyo.com/marketing-resources/abandoned-cart-benchmarks).
- Abandoned cart recovery read: flow conversions divided by abandon events, where abandon events are the count of the flow's trigger metric (Checkout Started or Started Checkout, whichever the brand uses) from metric-aggregates over the same dates; show the 10 to 15% band as the reference (https://attribuly.com/blogs/abandoned-cart-recovery-rate-klaviyo/).

### Row 5: Audience strip

- Email subscribers: total_members and net_members_changed series from segment-series-reports (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_segment_series); daily adds and removals from metric-aggregates on Subscribed to List and Unsubscribed from List, count, by List (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/supported_metrics_and_attributes).
- SMS subscribers: metric-aggregates on Subscribed to SMS Marketing and Unsubscribed from SMS Marketing, count by day (https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035345572).
- Forms: submits, submit_rate and viewed_form_uniques per form from POST /api/form-values-reports, with the 1 to 5% opt in band as the reference (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_form_values, https://flowium.com/blog/klaviyo-audit-expert-guide/).
- Revenue per subscriber for the month: Klaviyo attributed revenue (tile 1 numerator) divided by the email subscribers segment total_members, against the $1 and $1.50 to $3.00 bands (https://topgrowthmarketing.com/how-much-is-your-email-list-worth/).
- Engaged share: total_members of the brand's "engaged 90 days" segment divided by total subscribers, from segment-values-reports (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/reference/query_segment_values).

### Row 6: Deliverability detail (collapsed by default)

- Bounces, spam complaints and unsubscribes by inbox provider: metric-aggregates on Bounced Email, Marked Email as Spam and Unsubscribed, count, by Inbox Provider or Email Domain (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/supported_metrics_and_attributes). Opens and clicks by provider the same way on Opened Email and Clicked Email so a Gmail or Yahoo problem shows on its own row.
- SMS: failed_rate and unsubscribe_rate from campaign-values and flow-values filtered send_channel equals sms, against Klaviyo's SMS alert lines (click under 6.0%, unsubscribe over 1.3%) (https://help.klaviyo.com/hc/en-us/articles/21206358003355).
- Bounce reasons: metric-aggregates on Bounced Email by Bounce Type (https://developers.klaviyo.com/en/docs/supported_metrics_and_attributes).

- Not on the screen because the API does not return them: Klaviyo's peer benchmarks and percentiles, the deliverability score, predictive CLV roll ups, and the audience performance dashboard.
