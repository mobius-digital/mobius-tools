  • Benchmarks name their source and year on every line: Littledata Oct 2026 (Shopify stores, by device) for add to cart, checkout completion and conversion; Klaviyo 2026 for flow revenue per recipient. A step under its floor (1,000 sessions, or 300 carts or checkouts) says "not enough data yet".
  • A funnel step is a leak when it is more than 20% under its benchmark AND under the brand's own 90 days before. Cost = the orders it would add at the benchmark, carried down the brand's own funnel, x AOV.
  • Google, TikTok and video campaigns use the brand's own cut line (Ads rules), the one Meta's calls already use, on each platform's own conversions.
