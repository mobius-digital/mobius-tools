Read me first: what changed in v2, and your three questions

Everything opens

Every bar, drop pin, email dot, work row, Coming up card, tile and brand name opens something. Bars look like the Black Friday gantt already in Locus (outlined in their colour, solid = confirmed, striped = pencilled, dashed = no offer yet) and a label that does not fit sits beside its bar. Hover shows the dates and the offer. The month view moves with ‹ › (September to December).

How a sale ends

  • Every sale asks for an end date. If there isn't one, it shows "No end date" (dashed end) and a warning, like Burgundy's tiers.
  • Open-ended sales get one line in the Monday post: "still running?" with It ended today, one tap.
  • A sale that ends early: same button on its panel. Nothing else to remember.
  • Later, with the Mobius Shopify app on a store, the end comes straight from the Shopify discount.

Clients and Slack

  • No client link. Clients sign in to Locus (per-brand access already exists) and see their own calendar: screen 6. They add and move their own dates; we get pinged.
  • Until clients have Locus logins, Nick and Dartee keep using the old Lineup: it writes the same dates, so nothing is lost.
  • Screen 7 is what the messages look like IN Slack. They go to each brand's internal channel, already set in Brand settings; nothing new to connect.
