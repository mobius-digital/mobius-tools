Read me first: what this page is for, in one minute

The goal

The dates customers see drive the work that must be ready for them. Every drop or sale gets a countdown (assets, ads, emails, live) that ticks ITSELF from Asana, Klaviyo and Meta. Nobody keeps it tidy by hand: Season's tasks show why (none ever ticked) and so does Burgundy (Oct 7 was missed and nothing said so).

Who uses it, when

  • Clients add their drops and sales on their link. We get a Slack ping.
  • Ahsan and Noma, Monday: "Work due this week" and one click to make the Asana tasks.
  • Media buyer, daily: the day-before ping and what goes live today.
  • Cole, weekly: every client in one picture: what is coming, what clashes, who has nothing planned.
  • Everyone after: the date shows on the charts with "did it work".

What is real in this mock

  • Every date and offer: Season (live D1), Lineup (Nick's and Dartee's events), Lucky's Klaviyo sends, Dartee's sales by day (Triple Whale).
  • Burgundy's Asana state (7 tasks, Ready for Launch) is real.
  • Ticks marked "example" are illustrative: in the build they come from Asana, Klaviyo and Meta.
  • The rail follows the structure audit's proposal (not signed off yet).
