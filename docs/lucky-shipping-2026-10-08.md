## The $24 is per order that FLIPS, not per order
Moving the line only changes orders that sit between the old and new line. Everything below already
pays; everything above still ships free.

### Black Friday weekend, line $195 -> $199
Only one order flips: **3 Gold Carvers at $197**. 1 wedge ($99) and 2 wedges ($148) already pay;
anything with a Black Carver, putter or Stryker is $207+ and stays free. $198 and $199 are identical
on the weekend (no Black Friday order lands at $198).
- 1,000 weekend orders, maybe 80 to 150 of them 3 Gold Carvers -> at most **$1,900 to $3,600**.
- Buyers who balk at $25 mostly drop to 2 wedges ($95 left, same as 3), so that part is low risk.
- The risk is the headline losing "ships free" across the whole weekend: every 1% of weekend orders
  lost costs about $850 at 1,000 orders ($85 average left per order).

### Black Friday weekend, no free shipping at all
Every order over $195 flips: putters, Stryker, Blade, 3+ clubs. Guess 20 to 25% of weekend orders.
- 1,000 orders -> 200 to 250 flip -> at most **$4,800 to $6,000**, before lost sales.
- Breaks even if 1 in 7 to 1 in 9 of those buyers walk (they are the $158 to $206 orders).
- "$24 x 1,000 = $24,000" would only be true if every order shipped free today. Most already pay.

### Normal days, line $195/$198 -> $199
Only 2 Gold Carvers at $198 flip: about 4% of orders, ~16 a month at summer volume.
- At most **~$390 a month**; breaks even if 1 in 6 of those buyers walk.
- A one-week A/B test sees ~4 affected orders: too few to read. Not worth testing on its own.

### Normal days, no free shipping
~34% of orders shipped free this summer (~145 a month) -> at most **~$3,500 a month**; breaks even
if about 1 in 7 of those buyers walk. This is the only everyday version big enough to be worth a
real test (several weeks, compare conversion rate and AOV, not just orders).
