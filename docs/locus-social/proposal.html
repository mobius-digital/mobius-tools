Locus Social, in plain words

A new spot in Locus that tells you what to post, gets it made, and posts it for you. Two screens. We post straight to Lucky's accounts, so it costs about $5 to $10 a month. Slack does the reminding.

How it works

Four steps, one loop

SEEWatchFollow brands like Takomo, plus topics like "golf short game". Their new posts on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube land in one feed. Posts doing way better than that account's normal get flagged.
PICKMake oursClick a post. Claude writes Lucky's version as a shot-by-shot script (new angle, our product, never their footage) and sends it to Studio, the team or creators.
PLANWeekYour week as a time grid. Drag finished content from the tray onto a day. Monday the Strategist fills it with 5 posts for you to approve.
POSTAuto-postReady posts go out on their own, straight to Lucky's Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook. Views show on the card after.

Watch-outs

What could go wrong, and the fix built in

  • Approval waits. Filed on day one; Watch doesn't need them, and drafts or private uploads cover the gap.
  • Some brands won't load on Instagram (personal accounts). The follow box says so instead of showing an empty feed.
  • TikTok data comes from a paid scraper, the only part that costs money. If it breaks, the feed keeps working on Instagram and YouTube.
  • Golf is a small room. Make ours always changes angle and product and never reuses their footage.
  • AI can't fake Lucky's clubs. So the plan is 5 good posts a week from people and Studio statics, not 15.
Build plan

Three phases, each one useful on its own

1. Watch

live 2026-10-19
  • File the Meta, TikTok and Google approvals
  • Social in the rail, switch in Brand settings, Lucky on
  • Follow brands and topics; daily pull from Instagram, TikTok, YouTube
  • Cards: outlier number, hook and format tags, watch the original
  • Breaking out, Saved, trending strip
  • Make ours: script, then Studio, Work card or creator link

Done when: you click a breakout post and a script plus a Studio draft or Work card exists with the reference attached.

2. Week + post

built 2026-10-19 (waiting on the three grants)
  • Connect Lucky's accounts (one login each)
  • Hour grid, ready tray, composer with account bubbles
  • Auto-post direct; views on Posted cards
  • Winning ads and creator clips feed the tray
  • Posts on the Locus Calendar

Done when: a post scheduled in Locus goes live on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube with nobody touching it.

3. Strategist

last
  • Monday morning card: 5 posts for the week, Approve buttons
  • Breakout alerts in the morning card
  • Our own numbers steer the next plan

Done when: a Monday passes with 5 posts approved from Slack and nobody opened Locus.
