## 2. The category (Ad Library + web)

- **Hockey retail runs percent off.** 35% off clearance, up to 55% off a big gear brand, 15% sitewide codes on
  the league shop. Nobody in hockey apparel sells a gift with purchase, which is the point: Rob protects price
  and a free lid looks different in the feed.
- **Gift with purchase is native to hockey, just not apparel.** A big hockey retailer ran "free hat with a stick
  purchase" in its 2025 holiday guide. Our version is the same move on the apparel side.
- **The biggest hockey lifestyle brand on Meta (Violent Gentlemen, 63 ads in the library) sells culture, not
  price, in October:** "Hockey fans know where to shop", "A hockey brand built by hockey fans", "Your October
  uniform has arrived", "you're going to wear this one until it falls apart", catalog sets of hat + tee. That is
  the lane Yak's voice already owns, and Yak is funnier.
- **The lone snapback is a commodity picture.** The library is full of novelty light-up hockey snapbacks. A lid on
  its own looks like them; a lid with the hoodie on a person looks like a brand. Keep the pair in frame.
- **Open ground from the Locus research:** nobody dresses the parent in the stands, nobody says "hockey stank"
  out loud, the kid chooses and the parent pays, and the Hat Trick lid gets signed as a keepsake.
- Thin spot: Atria was rate-limiting today, so no ranked BFCM winners from the wider library. Rerun before Oct 16.

## 3. Rules for every line

- **Statics first.** Every line below is a static (4:5, then "Make 9:16").
- **Real photos, not drawn product.** Yak is not under Lucky's hard rule, but hoodie logos and lid embroidery are
  exactly what the model gets wrong. Use real product and model photos ("Use as the ad", or Dress a real person
  photo with real product photos). The red Playmaker + Hat Trick shot from the 2025 ad and the BFCM 2024 model
  shot are the two proven pictures; ask Yak for the originals.
- **Push the deep colorways, keep black out of the hero** (black M and L are low or out).
- Copy in the yak voice (Locus Brand > Voice: rink humour, short, a bit rude; "lids", "flow", THE HERD,
  CAN'T STOP THE YAK, hockey stank, product names as words). No em dashes. Never name another brand.
- Never a percent off. Value is shown as "$128 of gear for $78", never as a markdown.

Templates (from the Oct 9 analysis; `docs/bfcm-ad-templates.md` is not written yet):
T1 hero + one line, T2 feature callouts / grid, T3 scene + bullets, T4 price ladder (exact prices, Canva),
T5 moment wrapper (date, countdown, sticker), T6 review over product (real quotes only).

## 4. Early Black Friday: 15 ads, done by Oct 23, live Nov 6

Offer (proposal): buy any hoodie, pick a free snapback ($50 value). No markdown. The 5 FREE lines (11 to 15)
carry no offer, so they can be built now and also run as the **warm-up set from Oct 15 to Nov 5**: the build
calendar has no Yak warm-up batch, and the pixel needs three weeks of something before Nov 6.

1. **OFFER. The picture you already stopped for.** Why: Yak's most-seen ad ever (hoodie + Hat Trick lid,
   231 reactions, 2.5% CTR) sold at 0.7x because nothing made it a deal. Concept: the same red Playmaker + Hat
   Trick shot, one line: "Buy the hoodie. The lid's on us." Testing: does a free lid turn Yak's best-engaging
   picture into a seller. T1. Ref: own ad 120216108291260530.
2. **OFFER. Pick your lid.** Why: a choice feels bigger than a freebie, and the lid names (Hat Trick, Celly,
   Safety Meeting, Sticks, Olympic) are jokes people want to choose. Concept: hoodie in the middle, 8 lids around

3. **OFFER. $128 of rink for $78.** Why: Rob will not mark down; value math says "deal" without moving the price.

8. **OFFER. "I may have one in every color."** Why: real review, and 13 colorways is a reason to come back.
