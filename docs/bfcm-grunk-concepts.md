## Rules for every Grunk ad

- Statics first. One ad per line. Each line its own look so Meta reads them as different ads.
- The print is the hero: the loud print big in frame and named. Real beats staged. Jokes on top of a
  tiny product lose. Meme wrappers lost for us before (fake tweet 0.86, iMessage 1.12); a native format
  only when it reads like a real person and the polo sits right under it.
- Real photos first (Air library, "Use as the ad", or Dress on a real Air photo). Studio may draw Grunk
  products (the no-AI-product rule is Lucky only), but prints come out wrong often, so real pixels win.
- Parody lines: "no people, no human faces" in the brief, or the AI draws actor look-alikes.
- Drinking: beer in hand, casual. No chugging, no one looking drunk.
- Claims: only the site's (soft, 4-way stretch, moisture-wicking, breathable, relaxed dad bod fit,
  S to 4XL on every polo, 5XL on many). Never UPF on a polo, never hangover claims.
- No em dashes. No competitor names. No sales numbers in ads.
- 4:5, words and the main object inside the centre 1:1. No in-image buttons on feed ads.

## Batch 1: Lead list (15 ads, live Nov 1 to 25, done by Oct 18)

**The job:** names on the list before Thanksgiving, so the 24-hour deal lands on people who already said yes.
Last Thanksgiving made $17K on $392 of ads: the list did it.

**The promise:** join the list; on Thanksgiving the list gets 24 hours of polos at 3 for $99.

**Across the batch we test:** naming the price vs keeping it secret (lines 1 to 3 vs 4 and 5), and which
person the ad speaks to (lines 6 to 10), then Grunk-voice formats (11 to 15).

**Products:** Nov 2025 best sellers (Blue ChunkMan, Red ChunkMan, Red Hawaiian, Black Drinko, Happy Hour)
plus White Neon Rose (today's top polo). Real photos from Air wherever possible.

### Name the price

1. **The math**
   - Angle: $99 for three polos is the whole pitch.
   - Why: last year's 3 for $99 made the biggest day of the year; polos were 67% of November.
   - Concept: three real polos fanned out on a cart seat (Red ChunkMan, Blue ChunkMan, Black Drinko), prints
     filling the frame. Giant type: "3 POLOS. $99." Small: "Thanksgiving only. The list gets it first."
   - Testing: the price as the headline.
   - Template: giant offer type over real product.
   - Reference: 287-12 (print hero, Broad 2211 anchor); Bogey Bros 1564730007482926.

2. **The receipt**
   - Angle: do the math for him.
   - Why: "do the math" framing worked in the category, and $33 a polo is a number people repeat.
   - Concept: a real paper receipt clipped to a folded White Neon Rose polo. 3 x $69.95 = $209.85.
     "List price, Nov 26: $99.00." "You saved: $110.85." Headline: "$33 a polo. One day."
   - Testing: receipt math vs the big number (line 1).
   - Template: receipt.
   - Reference: Bad Birdie 1334684818139527.

3. **Hats on the list too**
   - Angle: the hat deal, for the guy who already owns ten polos.
   - Why: hats are the strong add-on (247 units in 60 days, the Chunkman hat restock); a hat lead gives the
     list a second reason.
   - Concept: a row of real Grunk hats on a bar top (Chunkman, Donde Esta La Cart Girl, Smashters), beer in
     a koozie at the end. Headline: "Hats, $89. Thanksgiving. List only." **(confirm what $89 buys)**
   - Testing: hat-led signup vs polo-led.
   - Template: product row with one headline.
   - Reference: Rudy Concepcion Navy Open Hat (only creator win, $16 CPA).

### Keep it secret

4. **The guest list**
   - Angle: the best deal we run all year goes to the list first.
   - Why: mystery makes people sign up to find out; VIP cards are the standard early-access ad.
   - Concept: velvet rope at a clubhouse door, a bouncer's clipboard reading "GRUNK LIST" with a polo hanging
     on the rope post. Tape banners: "List only" / "Thanksgiving" / "24 hours". No price.
   - Testing: secret vs named price (pairs with line 1).
   - Template: VIP early-access card.
   - Reference: Dore & Rose 1334224278159394; HeyShape 793155750375489.

5. **Thanksgiving, scheduled**
   - Angle: put it on your calendar between the turkey and the nap.
   - Why: a native screen reads like his own phone; a real-sounding line wins where our fake tweets lost.
   - Concept: phone calendar for Thu Nov 26: "Turkey 2pm", "Nap 4pm", "Grunk 24-hour deal (list only), all day".
     A real Red ChunkMan polo photo fills the lower half. Headline: "Clear your Thanksgiving."
   - Testing: native calendar format.
   - Template: phone screen over product.
   - Reference: Bogey Bros one-line text 2115617322635468.

### Who it speaks to

6. **The gift buyer**
   - Angle: he is impossible to buy for, so get him three.
   - Why: the gift buyer is the category's top ad lane and Grunk has never run it; reviews say
     "finally found something he didn't have".
   - Concept: a woman's hand resting on a stack of three folded Grunk polos on a bed, wrapping paper beside it.
     Headline: "He's impossible to buy for. He's getting three." Small: "3 polos, $99, Thanksgiving. Join the list."
   - Testing: the gift buyer's voice.
   - Template: hand on product, big caption.
   - Reference: Bogey Bros 2598276590530317; the September gift-buyer persona.

7. **The crew**
   - Angle: dress the whole foursome for the price of one polo each.
   - Why: crew orders are real ($330 to $400 baskets last summer) and the foursome shot is open in our ads.
   - Concept: four guys on a first tee in four different loud Grunk polos (real Air photo, Dress if needed),
     beers in koozies. Headline: "The whole foursome. $33 a polo." Small: "Thanksgiving. List only."
   - Testing: the group buy.
   - Template: real group photo with a headline.
   - Reference: The News Team (Burgundy batch 365); birddogs foursome walk 1138217752221924.

8. **The big guy**
   - Angle: the polo that fits a beer belly, at $33.
   - Why: our fit is the one thing others cannot claim; "small through five" and the beer-belly callouts are
     our best small-spend results (Tiktok #25 11.4 on $87, 284-1 4.91).
   - Concept: real polos laid flat from small to the biggest size, smallest to largest, left to right.
     Headline: "Dad bod approved. $33 a polo." Callouts: "Relaxed fit", "Up to 5XL on most prints".
   - Testing: fit as the reason to join.
   - Template: size lineup.
   - Reference: 284-1 callouts; Tiktok #25.

9. **The review**
   - Angle: nobody stops at one.
   - Why: review cards are our best format (3.32 average); "Tried 1... bought 3 more" is a real review.
   - Concept: plain review card, 5 stars, "Tried 1... bought 3 more." over a close-up of the Black Drinko print.
     Small: "On Thanksgiving, 3 is $99. Join the list."
   - Testing: review proof as the signup reason.
   - Template: review card over print close-up.
   - Reference: 338 C (3.39 to 3.60).

10. **Retire the boring ones**
   - Angle: three boring polos out, three Grunks in.
   - Why: "throw away your old polos" is our best angle by ROAS (3.19 across the angle, 338 C).
   - Concept: a closet rail. Three navy and beige polos on the left with a "DONATE" sticky note; three loud Grunk
     polos on the right. Headline: "Trade in the boring three. $99 on Thanksgiving."
   - Testing: anti-boring vs gift and crew.
   - Template: before and after split.
   - Reference: 338 C; the closet purge angle.

### Grunk voice

11. **Eating sport**
   - Angle: golf is a drinking sport, Thanksgiving is an eating sport.
   - Why: our own trademark line, never used on the holiday; ties the deal to the day.
   - Concept: a real Thanksgiving table, a Happy Hour polo draped over the chair at the head, beer beside the
     plate. Headline: "Golf is a drinking sport. Thanksgiving is an eating sport." Small: "Relaxed fit for
     second helpings. 3 polos $99, list only."
   - Testing: holiday humour.
   - Template: real scene, one headline.
   - Reference: Brown Chunkman "fuel up before you tee up" copy.

12. **Paid full price in October**
   - Angle: don't be the guy who paid $69.95.
   - Why: the two-character meme ran 367 days for another apparel brand; a cheap format test.
   - Concept: two panels. Left: "Guy who paid full price", a polo with a price tag. Right: "Guy on the Grunk list",
     three polos and a beer. No faces. Small: "Join the list for Thanksgiving."
   - Testing: meme format (flagged: our memes lost before; keep it as the small test).
   - Template: two-panel comparison.
   - Reference: Craftd 1238456744034464.

13. **The sticky note**
   - Angle: a note to self.
   - Why: 293 C (sticky note on a folded Drinko) is one of our steadiest stills (1.61 to 1.83).
   - Concept: a yellow sticky note on a folded Black Drinko polo: "Nov 26. Grunk list. 3 for $99. Don't
     tell the group chat." Nothing else.
   - Testing: a proven look carrying the new offer.
   - Template: sticky note on product (293 C).
   - Reference: 293 C.

14. **The warning label**
   - Angle: not for guys who like their navy polo.
   - Why: a negative hook breaks the "you need this" feed and lets him pick himself as the fun one.
   - Concept: a fake warning label on a polo tag: "WARNING: Do not join this list if you enjoy beige.
     Side effects include compliments at the turn." Headline: "The Grunk list. Thanksgiving."
   - Testing: negative hook.
   - Template: warning label close-up.
   - Reference: Bogey Bros reverse listicle 2108429963090261.

15. **Pick any three**
   - Angle: choice. The whole wall, any three, $99.
   - Why: the Collector owns 8 to 15 polos; a grid shows range the single-hero ads cannot.
   - Concept: a 3 by 3 grid of real print close-ups, each named small (White Neon Rose, Red ChunkMan, Black
     Drinko, Happy Hour, Desert Gamo, Patriot Chunkman, Red Hawaiian, Blue ChunkMan, Burgundy Drinko).
     Headline: "Pick any three. $99." Small: "Thanksgiving, list only."
   - Testing: range vs a single hero.
   - Template: product grid.
   - Reference: 287 series (print named big).

**Post copy (all 15):** "Thanksgiving, 24 hours, list only: polos 3 for $99. Join the Grunk list and we'll
send it to you first." Headline field: "Join the Grunk list." CTA: Sign Up.

**Studio cost:** about 45 cents an ad with fixes (Burgundy: 23 ads for about $11). 15 ads: $7 to $9.
Dress steps (lines 7, 8): about 11 cents each.
