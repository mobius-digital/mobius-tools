# Ice & Gold: creator-link research (2026-10-06)

Brand: Ice & Gold, iceandgold.com, @iceandgld. Atlanta GA. Founded Oct 2023 by Fela Johnson (wife, mom of 4). 18K gold-filled and gold PVD everyday jewelry. No creator page exists yet; this doc is the raw material for building it from scratch.

Owner rule that overrides everything below: never say "gold plated". Say gold-filled or gold PVD.

How this doc was built: Atria (resolve + top-video searches on Melinda Maria, Ana Luisa, JAXXON, Mejuri, Craftd, PAVOI, one library-wide "rising since Sept" sweep, one industry hook sweep, 15 free transcripts), Motion ad-library summaries for Craftd, JAXXON, Mejuri, Ana Luisa, Melinda Maria, GLD, BaubleBar, Oak & Luna, and web reading of reviews (Trustpilot, Loox, Reviewed.com, Refinery29, Greenlit) plus the Ice & Gold site itself. ## 2. Patterns: the shapes that keep showing up

1. **Founder trunk show.** Founder holds one piece, names it, says it is the best seller, shows the clasp, says what outfit it fixes. Melinda Maria m1347251910609008 ("Uh, excuse me. This is our best selling collection of all time"), m971922298946421, m1219570106824927. Why it works: a named human with authority answers the first question a jewelry buyer has ("is this brand real?") in the first second, and the piece is in frame the whole time so there is never a cut away from the product.

2. **The green-neck confession.** Open on the pain, then the fix. Craftd m1073914602481069 ("Has it ever turned your neck green before?" "Yeah."), JAXXON m1541606723604426 ("Never wear a chain like this unless you want your skin to turn green"), JAXXON Motion "won't give you that embarrassing green neck". Why: it is the category's universal, slightly embarrassing memory. Naming it in second one earns the next 5 seconds, and the fix (tarnish free, sweat proof) is Ice & Gold's whole claim set.

3. **The torture diary.** "I have not taken it off since. I work out in this, I shower in this, I jump in the ocean in this" (Oak & Luna m1417233863835377); "He slept in it that night, worked out in it the next morning... Three months later, no fading" (JAXXON m1362619162625683); the JAXXON 30-day test format (Motion); Refinery29's two-week no-removal test of a tarnish-proof brand. Why: time plus a list of abuses is proof you can picture, and the viewer waits for the reveal at the end.

4. **Looks expensive, is not.** "I found the most expensive looking necklace all the celebs are wearing... luxury is a feeling, not a price tag" (Melinda Maria m975099272002734), "The weight and shine alone felt way more expensive than what I paid for it" (JAXXON m1362619162625683), Melinda Maria "Guess the Price?" title, Craftd "$100 FOR UNNECESSARY PACKAGING?!" and "DON'T GET RIPPED OFF" comparisons. Why: a price guess is an open loop, and weight-and-finish talk gives the viewer a way to judge quality without a jeweler.

5. **The gift reveal told by the giver.** JAXXON m1362619162625683 is the clean version: wife buys, checks it early, engraved initials on the clasp, "He opened it, went quiet for a second, and just put it on." Olivia & Pearl m2154889298715714 ("sold out last year, don't miss out on the perfect gift"). Oak & Luna's "best friends gave it to me for my 30th" street interview (Motion). Why: the giver's anxiety (will they like it?) is the story, and the quiet-then-puts-it-on beat is the payoff. Works for mothers, husbands, sisters.

6. **Help me decide.** "you're gonna help me decide which ones I want to wear" (Mejuri m4627339537545386), "Start with my best seller" (Melinda Maria), Ana Luisa "How did I not know about these earrings before?!" Why: a choice between two pieces keeps people watching to see which wins, and it shows two SKUs without feeling like a catalog.

7. **Street interview.** Craftd m1073914602481069 (man outside gym), Craftd "Do you like chains on men?" (women asked about men's jewelry), Oak & Luna "what is she wearing" interview. Why: strangers' faces reacting are a rehook every 3 seconds, and a woman saying she likes a chain on a man sells the man's chain to the woman who will buy it.

8. **Never take it off / one set for everything.** Ana Luisa m1871996997130595 ("they literally survive everything... comfortable to sleep in"), Craftd m1051057737595285 ("You shouldn't have to take it off"), JAXXON "CHAINS YOU NEVER HAVE TO TAKE OFF", Mejuri "Low maintenance. High shine." Why: it reframes the purchase from decoration to removing a daily chore (taking jewelry off), which is a reason to buy that the viewer did not have before.

9. **Personalised letter as the gift.** Melinda Maria m971922298946421 and m1219570106824927 (letter medallions), Oak & Luna m1417233863835377 (three boys' initials), Oak & Luna "If it isn't personal, it isn't yours", JAXXON's engraved clasp. Why: a letter makes the piece about a person, and gifts for a person are easier to justify than jewelry for a look. Ice & Gold has the Gold Letter Pendant with Cuban Chain (unisex, A to Z, $89) and the Initial Pave Pendant ($59).

10. **Warranty and review count in the title, compliments in the mouth.** Ana Luisa "2-Year Warranty. 50,000+ 5-Star Reviews.", JAXXON "100,000+ 5-Star Reviews... GQ voted it the best men's chain", Melinda Maria "LIFETIME GUARANTEE". Why: the guarantee removes the last objection without taking up seconds of the video. Ice & Gold's 1-year warranty and free US shipping belong in the title and on-screen text, not in the script.

11. **Podcast clip.** JAXXON m1513411913702715 ("Twitch, what's the only chain you wear when you step out?"). Why: two men talking about a chain is the only format where a man admits he cares about jewelry. Useful for Babs.

12. **Silent product loop.** Craftd's longest runner m1501609804258402 (299 days, 11s, music only), Mejuri "Low maintenance. High shine." 5s loops. Why: they work as retargeting and catalog reminders at tiny cost. Note our own "BEST SELLING Catalog" (2.77x) is this shape and it held, while agency statics did not. Not a creator format, but worth one line on the page so creators do not try to be this.
