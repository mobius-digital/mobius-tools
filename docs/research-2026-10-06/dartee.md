## 2. Patterns (the shapes that keep showing up)

1. **The click.** Anson's number one ad opens on the sound and the sentence "once I put that on though I heard the [click]" (m2028969921084508). The creator rant ends on "none of this seems revolutionary. It just seems like the way belts should have worked all along" (m1980515675946741). The mechanism is the hook because a ratchet makes a noise and a hole does not. Why it works: a sound in the first second is a pattern interrupt that costs nothing, and the viewer can verify it with their eyes. Dartee has a louder version of this: the marker snapping onto the magnet.

2. **Holes are the villain.** Three of Anson's top creator videos are confessions about holes: "poke holes into my belt ... sloppy mess of homemade holes" (m1622172212779618), "One hole is too tight, one hole is too loose. And if you want something in between, go buy a belt punch." (m1980515675946741), "holes in my belt get all stretched out" (m1730602779068185). Groove: "No holes. No digging in." Why it works: everybody has a belt with a stretched hole; it is a shared, low-stakes pain that makes the viewer nod before the product shows up. For Dartee this is the September "Cut it once" and "Ditch the dad belt" lane, now with better openers.

3. **"I might suck at golf, but my gear doesn't."** Pins and Aces' WITB creator (m1651267819953460) and the fit-check creator (m2302755733814212: "shoot a hundred and still get compliments", "looking like a jamoke") both lead with self-deprecation then "look good, play good". Why it works: it disarms the shill objection in the first line (the creator is not claiming the gear improves the score) and gives the average golfer permission to buy for looks. Dartee's "3 strokes minimum" and "white belt handicap" jokes belong here.

4. **Price reluctance, then the reason.** The TikTok Shop bag creator says the number first: "priced at 399 and ... I really wasn't ready to pay that kind of money" (m1067339445915203). Our own best-CPA ad in September did the same ("$300 for a golf belt?", 120239237680840741). Why it works: saying the objection out loud buys the next ten seconds; the viewer stays to see if the answer is good.

5. **The partner films the reveal.** Bogey Bros' wife video (m1859794814679897, 192 days) is a single take: setup line, hand it over, wait for the realization. Why it works: the viewer is waiting for one specific beat (does he notice?), which is the simplest possible retention device. Golf widow TikTok is the same voice: "On Wednesdays, I'm a golf widow." (@allyssainthekitchen, via distractify.com/p/what-is-a-golf-widow-tiktok).

6. **Skeptic unboxing.** Sunday Golf runs a review as the whole hook: "I was a bit skeptical, but that changed quickly when I opened the box." (m4380940345480146). Why it works: the doubt is stated by a customer, not the brand, and the box answers it. Dartee's "Felt like I was opening a luxury present" review (Sept doc) is the same device.

7. **New line, held to the lens.** Anson's ostrich launch (m1102646692210777) is a founder holding the belt, naming the texture, naming one colour, "it's not out yet, but by the time you're seeing this, it should be." Why it works: texture is a visual hook on its own, and a named drop gives a reason to film this week. Dartee has 50 Shades of Gator, Bone Dry and Purple Reign as new right now.

8. **The self-aware brag.** Groove: "Did I mention the groove belt is the best belt in the world? ... I can't stop doing this." (m957958250525755). Why it works: it makes the claim and mocks the claim in one breath, so the viewer cannot roll their eyes first. Dartee is allowed to say "#1 belt in golf"; this is how to say it without sounding like an ad.

9. **"Same belt since 8th grade."** Groove's 10-second cut (m1009061838517746) and Anson's "you're probably seeing these belts everywhere" (m1575988264254100) both talk straight to the guy who has never thought about his belt. Why it works: it names the viewer, not the product.

10. **One product, said plainly, for a year.** Sunday Golf's Big Rig (576 days) and first-outing creator (356 days). Why it works: a video that says exactly what the thing is and shows it used once can run forever; that evergreen slot is still open for a golf belt (September's "What nobody is doing" list still holds).

## 3. Visual hooks and rehooks seen

Physical openers (0 to 2 seconds):
- Buckle to the lens, then the ratchet click as the first sound (Anson m2028969921084508).
- Creator holding up an old belt with a row of homemade holes, flat to camera (Anson m1980515675946741, implied by the script; the preview frame is the creator to camera).
- Mirror fit check, full outfit, belt at centre frame (Anson m1622172212779618, m1730602779068185).
- New belt held in both hands, texture filling the frame, colour named out loud (Anson m1102646692210777).
- Bag on the floor, box lid already off, hand going straight to a pocket (Pins and Aces m1067339445915203, m1612167023405933).
- Creator in full golf fit on the course, pointing at pieces of the outfit as he names them (Pins and Aces m2302755733814212).
- Hat held to the lens, then turned to show the magnet with the marker on it (Pins and Aces m1763143554895344).
- Wife to camera holding the wrapped gift, husband not yet in frame (Bogey Bros m1859794814679897).
- Review text as the first frame (Sunday Golf m4380940345480146, Groove m1785245499157080).
- Old belt dropped in a toilet (our own 120240009827450741, still 2.72x; nobody else in the category is doing a physical pattern interrupt).
- Box opened flat with every piece visible (our own 120237392010370741, the workhorse).

Rehooks (3 to 10 seconds, the thing that keeps them):
- "Then I found this." A hard cut from the old belt to the new one (Anson m1980515675946741 at 17s).
- The price number said out loud, then a lower number (Pins and Aces m1067339445915203: 399, 297, 249).
- A joke about the brand's own claim (Groove m957958250525755 repeats "best belt in the world" three times).
- The realization beat: the husband says "Wait a second." (Bogey Bros m1859794814679897 at 16s). The whole video is built to reach that line.
- Pocket-by-pocket count with the hand on each one (Pins and Aces WITB m1651267819953460; Sunday Big Rig m581226258294725 "12 thoughtfully designed pockets").
- A warning that doubles as a joke: "just make sure you don't put the weight back on because you cannot reattach the part of the belt that you cut off" (Anson m1622172212779618 at 62s).
- Scarcity said casually at the end: "If you see a style that you like, I wouldn't wait long because this stuff goes super quick." (Pins and Aces m2302755733814212).

Not seen anywhere in the sweep, and still open for Dartee: the belt in a real round (bending to read a putt, marking a ball, the swing), the glove clipped on and off between shots, a buddy noticing the belt across 18 holes.
