# SpeedIn creator link: video research (2026-10-17)

SpeedIn has no ads or data yet, so the whole board is built from other brands' videos. We copy the format, not the product. Every pick below was downloaded and checked frame by frame (contact sheet every 1 to 2.5 seconds) for the phone rule.

## What I looked at

- **NuStrips** (the closest match on the market: energy, sleep and essentials strips that melt on the tongue, same "no pills, no water" pitch). About 12 of their top ads, including creator partner ads. They also run TRYBE links.
- **Strip and patch adjacent:** The Patch Remedy (energy patch), Restelle (sleep patch), Hostage Tape (mouth tape), Rejuvia (sleep spray), Flamingo (magnesium strips, only seen in search).
- **Energy and coffee:** Everyday Dose, Ankhway, Tradesman Nutrition, Pique, My Wellaray, Tropeaka.
- **Sleep:** Winks, Manta Sleep, Budpop, 8hours, Hears.
- **Hair, skin, nails:** Obvi, MaryRuth's, Hairburst, NativePath, Pique, Cowboy Colostrum, Theweem.
- **Format argument:** Grüns, AG1, Emmafy, Pure Genius Protein, Shameless Snacks, Organised Nutrition.
- Stripit (getstripit) is not in Atria's library (both name lookups came back "not found").

## What wins

- **The strip on the tongue in the first seconds.** NuStrips' top energy ads open on a hand peeling the pouch or a strip held up to the lens. That shot alone explains the product.
- **Car selfies.** Three of the strongest (NuStrips man with the can, Rejuvia, and NuStrips sleep) are filmed in a parked car. Easy light, no setup.
- **A clock or a time.** "It's 2:56 PM" (Patch Remedy) and the 5:30am alarm (Ankhway) hook before anyone speaks.
- **Swapping out the old thing.** Can, coffee build, pill bottles, powders. Showing the old way makes the strip look easy without saying "easy".
- **Two person and comedy beats.** Grüns' couple skit and Hostage's "hacks have gone too far" do the format argument better than any list of benefits.
- **Someone else noticed.** For hair and nails, a stylist pointing at new growth or nails held up to the lens beats talking about ingredients.

## Checklist draft

**Before you film**
1. **Film it on your phone.** Vertical, natural light, your real car, kitchen or bedroom. No editing apps beyond captions.
2. **Show the strip in the first 3 seconds.** Strip held to the lens or going on your tongue. That shot does the explaining.
3. **Use the product for real first.** Try it for a few days so what you say is how it felt to you.

**Say it right**
- Say how it felt to you ("no jitters for me"), never what it does for everyone.
- Sleep strips are for bedtime only. Never film them with a drink or before driving.
- Name SpeedIn only. Hide other brands' logos and never name them.

**Skip these**
- Cures, treats, fixes, doctor recommended or FDA approved. None of it.
- Prices, discounts or codes unless we send you one.
- Kids, pregnancy or nursing. These strips are for adults.
