# Lineup

**What it is.** A marketing calendar for e-commerce brands run by Mobius
Digital. One deployment, every client at `/b/<brand>/`. It answers three
questions: what is going live and when, where each launch is in its prep, and
which channel (paid, email, organic, SMS) has work to do about it.

**Who uses it.** A brand's marketing team (two to six people), the Mobius team
working across several brands, and the owner checking on a phone between
meetings. Nobody is a power user; most sessions are under two minutes.

**Register.** Product. Design serves the task: the tool should disappear into
the calendar.

**The physical scene.** A laptop on a Monday morning with a coffee, the
calendar shared on a video call; and the same board on an iPhone on the way to
a photo shoot. Bright rooms, glanced at, not stared into. That forces light.

**Views.**
- Calendar (month, week, agenda). Chips run from teaser start to promo end,
  colored by stage, with a dot on launch day. Drag a chip to move a launch.
- Board. One column per stage; drag a card to say what it is now waiting on.
- Changelog. Every edit, automatically, with a name against it.
- All brands (agency view). Every board's calendar on one page.

**Two fields people confuse.** *Status* is whether the date is real
(confirmed, tentative, at risk, completed, cancelled). *Stage* is what the
team is waiting on (briefed, waiting on assets, built, scheduled...) and is
editable per brand.

**What it is not.** Not a task manager and not a product-development
tracker. Internal work that produces assets lives in Asana; the calendar
holds what a customer can see on a date.
