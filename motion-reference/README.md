# The reference spot

tally-reference.mp4: a 53-second concept commercial Cole found (a "Tally" money app, made with Claude and
Remotion by its author; not our work, kept here only as the reference for our own spots). sheet_0 to sheet_3
are one frame per second as contact sheets. The beats and why it works are in docs/two-products.md section 1.
What Cole wants from it, in his words (2026-10-11): "it was showing cards that were actually in the app
itself, making it real and true, like they were in the dashboard and it was showing numbers; you could see
ad spend bleeding, melt down, graphs, bar charts, everything in real motion; a voiceover and sound."
