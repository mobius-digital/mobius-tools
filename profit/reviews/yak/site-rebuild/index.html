Are we using Yak's design system?

Yes. Kept, fixed, then extended.

Yak has a system in its theme today: the black and gold logo, Montserrat, black text, Yak gold buttons. The problem is it's applied loosely: gold, navy, purple and red buttons all at once, 14px rounded corners, and headlines baked into images. The new system keeps everything Yak owns and tightens it. My first round of mockups used colors I picked, not Yak's. Everything below is redone in Yak's real system.

Product cards + photos

Portrait photo on ice gray · one corner tag (Best seller / New / Free beanie) · round + quick add on the photo · name and price on one line · one "feel" line · color dots with +N. No more gold Shop Now + eye button under every card. Lifestyle photos (couples, kids, the rack of colors) go in the hero, the "Can't decide?" tile and one tile every 8 to 12 products. None of the five competitors does lifestyle tiles inside the grid, so it's how Yak's grid looks different.

Voice (from Yak's own guide)

Yak backs the athlete, not the spotlight. It talks like the guy in the locker room who's been around: short sentences, a bit rude, one joke per piece, rink words used without explaining them (lids, flow, hockey stank, the Herd). Never "elevate your game," never corporate filler, never "you're already great." Every claim backed by something real: a review, a spec, the founding story.
