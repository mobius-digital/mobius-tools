Locus, by Mobius Digital. Last updated October 20, 2026.

In one read

Locus is the planning and reporting tool Mobius Digital runs for the brands it works with. A brand connects its own accounts (its store, ad accounts and social profiles) so Locus can read their numbers and, where the brand asks for it, publish the brand's own posts. The brand stays the owner of everything it connects and can disconnect at any time.

1. Who can use Locus

Locus is for Mobius Digital's team and for the brands Mobius Digital works with, under their services agreement with Mobius Digital. A brand's people sign in with their own Google account and see only their own brand.

2. Connected accounts

When you connect an account (Shopify, Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube, Klaviyo or another service) you confirm you are allowed to connect it on the brand's behalf. Locus uses the access only to read that account's data for the brand and, where you have turned posting on, to publish content you scheduled in Locus to the brand's own profiles. You can remove a connection from Brand settings at any time; the stored token is deleted.

3. Posting on your behalf

Posts go out only when someone at the brand or at Mobius Digital schedules them in Locus. You are responsible for the content of those posts and for having the rights to the media in them. Each platform's own terms apply to what is published there.

4. Your data

How Locus handles data is described in the privacy policy. Numbers and files belong to the brand; Mobius Digital uses them to do the work the brand hired it for and for nothing else.

5. Availability and changes

Locus is provided as is. We work to keep it up and accurate, but the figures it shows come from third-party platforms and can be late or revised by them. We may change or retire features; we tell affected brands first when a change matters to them.

6. Ending use

A brand can stop using Locus at any time by disconnecting its accounts or asking Mobius Digital to remove it. Mobius Digital can suspend access that breaks these terms or a platform's rules.
