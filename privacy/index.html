Privacy Policy

Mobius Profit — Shopify application · Last updated 23 August 2026

Mobius Profit is a private reporting tool built and operated by Mobius Digital for the ecommerce brands we work with. It reads data from a connected Shopify store so we can report on that store's revenue, costs and customer economics. It is not listed on the Shopify App Store and is not offered to the general public.

The short version

  • We read orders, customers and products from your store. We never write to it.
  • We do not store individual customer records. Customer data is aggregated into monthly totals as it is processed; names, emails, addresses and phone numbers are never written to our database.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it to train models.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately stops all access. Your data is deleted within 30 days, or sooner on request.

Who we are

Mobius Digital. Contact cole@go-mobius-digital.com for any question about this policy, or to request access to or deletion of your data. For the stores we work with, Mobius Digital acts as a data processor and the store owner remains the data controller.

What the app accesses

When you install the app you grant three read-only permissions:

  • read_orders — order totals, dates, discounts, returns, shipping and tax, so we can report revenue and contribution margin.
  • read_customers — order counts and first-order dates, so we can report how many customers are new versus returning and how customer value develops over time.
  • read_products — product and variant costs, so we can report gross margin.

The app requests no write permissions and cannot modify, create or delete anything in your store.

What we actually store

This is the part most policies leave vague, so to be specific. We store:

  • Your store's myshopify.com domain and the access token issued at install.
  • Aggregated figures only — monthly totals such as revenue, ad spend, order counts, and the number of customers who first purchased in a given month together with their combined spend.

We do not store customer names, email addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or individual order or customer records. Customer-level data is read from Shopify, aggregated in memory, and only the totals are written down.

Where it is stored, and for how long

Data is held in Cloudflare D1 (a managed SQL database) and processed by Cloudflare Workers. Access is restricted to Mobius Digital staff who need it. Aggregated reporting data is retained while the app is installed and for up to 30 days afterwards, then deleted.

Who else sees it

Your data is visible to Mobius Digital staff working on your account, and to you. We use a small number of infrastructure providers to operate the service — Cloudflare for hosting and storage, Slack for delivering reports to the channel you nominate, and Anthropic for generating the written commentary in daily reports. These providers process data on our instructions only.

We do not sell or rent your data, do not share it with advertisers, and do not use it to train machine-learning models.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to or restrict how it is processed. Email cole@go-mobius-digital.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Shopify also relays privacy requests to us automatically. We receive and act on Shopify's customers/data_request, customers/redact and shop/redact webhooks. Because we hold no customer-level personal data, there is normally nothing to return or erase in response to a customer request; shop data is deleted on a shop redaction.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling the app from your Shopify admin revokes the access token immediately and we stop reading your store at once. Remaining aggregated data is deleted within 30 days, or straight away if you email us.

Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS. Access tokens are held as server-side secrets and are never exposed to a browser. Every webhook from Shopify is verified with an HMAC signature and rejected if it does not match.

Children

The service is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and notify connected stores by email.

This policy describes how the application handles data. It is not legal advice; store owners should satisfy themselves that it meets their own obligations.
