Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Mobius Digital LLC is an ad agency. We built Locus and a few related tools to run ads for the brands we work with. This page explains what data our tools get from Meta (Facebook and Instagram), what we do with it, and how to have it deleted. Our tools are private. They are used by our team and our clients, not sold to the public.

The short version

  • We only touch the ad accounts and Pages a client has given us access to.
  • We use that data for one thing: running and reporting on that client's own ads.
  • We never sell it, rent it, share it with advertisers, or use it to train AI models.
  • You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing cole@go-mobius-digital.com.

What we read from Meta

  • Ad account performance. Spend, impressions, clicks, purchases and other results for campaigns, ad sets and ads.
  • Ads and creatives. Ad names, status, budgets, copy, images, videos and their settings.
  • Pages and Instagram accounts linked to a client's ad account. Basic details (name, id, picture) and the posts used in ads, so we can show and build the ads correctly.
  • Business assets. Which ad accounts, Pages and catalogs a client has shared with our business, so we only work on what we were given.

We do not read personal profiles, friends lists or private messages, or anything about the people who see the ads beyond the totals Meta reports.

What we change in Meta

When our team or the client asks us to, our tools can create, edit, pause and resume campaigns, ad sets, ads and creatives in that client's ad account. We only do this on accounts the client has shared with us, and only to run that client's own ads.

Where the data is kept

Data is stored and processed on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2). Access tokens are kept as server side secrets and never sent to a browser. All traffic uses HTTPS. Only Mobius Digital staff working on an account, and that client, can see its data.

Who else handles it

A few service providers help us run the tools, and they handle data only on our instructions: Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Slack (sending reports to the client's channel) and Anthropic (writing the plain English summaries in our reports). We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share it with other advertisers.

How long we keep it

We keep ad data while we work with a client, so reports can compare against past months. When a client stops working with us, or asks us to delete it, we delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your data

Email cole@go-mobius-digital.com. The full steps are on our data deletion page. A client can also remove our access at any time in Meta Business Settings, which stops our tools at once.

Children

Our tools are for businesses and are not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
