Data Deletion

Last updated 16 October 2026

You can ask Mobius Digital to delete the data our tools hold about you or your business at any time. Here is how.

How to ask

  • Email cole@go-mobius-digital.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  • Say which business, ad account or Facebook Page it is about, so we delete the right data.
  • We reply to confirm we got it, and again when it is done.

What gets deleted

  • Ad performance data we copied from Meta (spend, results and history).
  • Copies of ads, creatives, images and videos we stored.
  • Details of the Pages, Instagram accounts and ad accounts linked to you.
  • Reports, notes and summaries our tools made from that data.
  • Any access tokens or connection details for your accounts.

Ads that already run inside your own Meta ad account belong to you and stay there. You can delete those in Meta Ads Manager.

How long it takes

We delete everything within 30 days of your request, usually much sooner. If the law makes us keep something (an invoice, for example), we tell you what and why.

Stop access right away

You can also cut off our access yourself at any time: in Meta Business Settings, remove Mobius Digital as a partner on your ad accounts and Pages. Our tools stop reading and changing your accounts at once.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
